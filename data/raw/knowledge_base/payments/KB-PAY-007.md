# Corporate Purchase Orders

## Overview

Business and Enterprise account holders can pay via Purchase Order (PO) for qualifying orders, enabling invoice-based billing on Net-30 or Net-60 terms.

## Eligibility

To use PO payment:

- Must have an active Business or Enterprise account.
- Credit limit must be approved by Acme Store Finance.
- Account must be in good standing (no overdue balances).

## How to Submit a Purchase Order

1. Add items to cart and proceed to checkout.
2. Select **Purchase Order** as payment method.
3. Enter your PO number in the designated field.
4. Upload the signed PO document (PDF format).
5. Submit the order. It will be reviewed within 4 business hours.

## PO Payment Terms

| Account Type | Payment Terms | Credit Limit |
|-------------|---------------|--------------|
| Business | Net-30 | Up to $25,000 |
| Enterprise | Net-30 or Net-60 | Up to $500,000 |

Enterprise clients may negotiate custom terms with their account manager.

## Invoicing

After order shipment:

- A formal invoice is issued within 24 hours.
- Invoice is sent to the billing contact on file.
- Invoice includes PO number for easy reconciliation.

## Late Payment Policy

Payments not received by the due date:

- A 1.5% monthly late fee applies after a 7-day grace period.
- Orders may be paused for accounts with overdue balances.
- Repeated late payments may result in removal of PO privileges.

## Bank Transfer Details

For PO payments via bank transfer:

- Routing Number: 123456789 (synthetic)
- Account Number: 987654321 (synthetic)
- Reference: Always include invoice number
