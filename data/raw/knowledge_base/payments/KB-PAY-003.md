# Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) Options

## Overview

Acme Store partners with select BNPL providers to allow customers to split their purchases into installments.

## Available BNPL Partners

| Provider | Available At Checkout | Min Order | Max Order |
|----------|-----------------------|-----------|-----------|
| PayLater  | acmestore.com | $50 | $3,000 |
| SplitPay | acmestore.com | $100 | $5,000 |
| MonthlyEase | In-store only | $200 | $10,000 |

## Payment Plans

### 4-Payment Plan (Interest-Free)

Split your purchase into 4 equal payments every 2 weeks.

- First payment due at checkout.
- Remaining 3 payments charged automatically.
- **No interest** if all payments are on time.
- Eligibility: All orders $50–$1,000.

### 12-Month Financing

For orders over $500, apply for 12-month financing:

- Monthly payments based on purchase price divided by 12.
- **0% APR** for first 6 months; standard APR applies for months 7–12.
- Credit check required (soft pull, no impact on credit score for prequalification).

## Eligibility Requirements

To use BNPL at Acme Store:

- Must be 18 years or older.
- Valid US bank account or debit card required.
- Acme Store account must be at least 30 days old.
- No outstanding missed BNPL payments on record.

## Missed Payment Policy

If a scheduled BNPL payment fails:

- A late fee of $10 applies after a 3-day grace period.
- Three missed payments result in the full remaining balance becoming due immediately.
- Repeated defaults may result in loss of BNPL eligibility.

## Returns with BNPL Orders

If a BNPL order is returned:

- Remaining payments are cancelled immediately.
- Already-made payments are refunded to the original payment source.
- BNPL provider processing may take 5-7 additional business days.
