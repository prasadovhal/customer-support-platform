# Gift Cards and Promotional Vouchers

## Gift Cards

### Purchasing Gift Cards

Acme Store gift cards are available in denominations of $25, $50, $100, $250, and $500.

- **Digital gift cards**: Delivered by email within 30 minutes of purchase.
- **Physical gift cards**: Shipped via standard mail (allow 5-7 business days).

Gift cards can be purchased at checkout or from the **Gift Cards** section of the website.

### Using Gift Cards

At checkout:

1. Select **Gift Card** as a payment method.
2. Enter the 16-digit gift card code.
3. Enter the 4-digit PIN (printed on the card or emailed).
4. The balance is applied to your order.

Multiple gift cards can be used for a single order. If the gift card balance is insufficient, the remainder is charged to your other payment method.

### Checking Gift Card Balance

Visit acmestore.com/giftcard or call 1-800-ACME-123.

### Gift Card Policy

- Gift cards do not expire.
- Cannot be exchanged for cash (except where required by law).
- Lost or stolen gift cards cannot be replaced unless a purchase receipt is provided.
- Acme Store is not responsible for unauthorized use of gift card codes.

## Promotional Vouchers and Discount Codes

### Types of Promotional Codes

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Percentage discount | e.g., "SAVE20" — 20% off |
| Fixed dollar discount | e.g., "SUMMER10" — $10 off |
| Free shipping | e.g., "FREESHIP" — removes shipping cost |
| Category-specific | e.g., valid only for electronics |

### How to Apply a Voucher

At checkout, enter the code in the **Promo Code** field. Discount is applied immediately to the eligible items.

### Voucher Restrictions

- One voucher per order (unless otherwise specified).
- Cannot be combined with other promotional offers.
- Minimum order amount may apply.
- Expiration dates are shown in the voucher details.
