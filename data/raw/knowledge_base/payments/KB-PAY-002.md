# Failed Payment Resolution

**Version 2.0 — Effective January 1, 2026**

## Overview

Payment failures can occur for several reasons. This document helps customers understand why payments fail and how to resolve them.

## Common Causes of Payment Failure

| Cause | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Insufficient funds | Card has insufficient balance |
| Incorrect card details | Wrong card number, expiry, or CVV |
| Address mismatch | Billing address does not match bank records |
| Card declined by bank | Bank has flagged the transaction |
| Daily spending limit | Transaction exceeds card's daily limit |
| International restrictions | Card not enabled for international purchases |
| Expired card | Card expiration date has passed |
| Account frozen | Card issuer has placed a hold on the account |

## Steps to Resolve a Failed Payment

### Step 1: Verify card details

Double-check that all card information is entered correctly:
- Card number (no spaces)
- Expiration date (MM/YY)
- CVV/CVC (3 or 4 digits depending on card type)
- Billing address exactly as it appears on your card statement

### Step 2: Contact your bank

If card details are correct, contact your card issuer. Banks sometimes decline unusual or large transactions as a fraud prevention measure. Ask them to authorize the transaction.

### Step 3: Try an alternative payment method

If the issue persists, try a different payment method such as PayPal or a different card.

### Step 4: Contact Acme Store support

If none of the above resolves the issue, contact support at billing@acmestore.example with your order number (if available) and the error message you received.

## Duplicate Charge Concerns

If you see multiple charges for the same order:

1. Check if one is a pending authorization and one is the actual charge — the authorization hold should drop off within 3–7 days.
2. If both appear as settled charges, contact Acme Store immediately at billing@acmestore.example.

Duplicate charges are resolved within 5 business days with a full refund of the duplicate amount.

## Order Status During Payment Failure

If your payment fails, the order will not be confirmed. The items will be held in your cart for 24 hours. You may retry payment from your account or the cart.

After 24 hours, items return to general inventory (subject to availability).
