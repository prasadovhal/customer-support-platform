# Tax, Invoices, and Receipts

## Sales Tax

Acme Store collects sales tax in accordance with applicable state and local laws. Tax rates vary by:

- Delivery address (state/county/city).
- Product type (some items are tax-exempt in certain states).

The applicable tax is calculated and shown at checkout before payment is confirmed.

### Tax-Exempt Purchases

If you or your organization qualifies for a tax exemption:

1. Create a Business or Enterprise account.
2. Upload your tax-exempt certificate in **Account Settings** > **Tax Exemption**.
3. Once verified (1-3 business days), future orders will be tax-exempt.
4. Retroactive tax refunds are not available for orders placed before certificate approval.

## Order Receipts

An email receipt is automatically sent to your registered email address after each order is placed. The receipt includes:

- Order number
- Itemized product list
- Subtotal, shipping cost, tax, and total
- Payment method (last 4 digits)
- Estimated delivery date

To view past receipts: go to **Account** > **Order History** > select an order > **Download Receipt**.

## Invoices for Business Accounts

Business and Enterprise account holders can request formal invoices (with company name, VAT number, and line-item details):

1. Go to **Account** > **Billing** > **Request Invoice**.
2. Enter your company details and VAT/EIN number.
3. Invoice is generated within 24 hours and emailed in PDF format.

Invoices include:
- Acme Store company details (seller information)
- Buyer company details
- Line items with HSN/SAC codes
- Tax breakdown by line item

## VAT for International Orders

For orders shipping outside the US, VAT may be applied based on the destination country's requirements. Acme Store acts as the importer of record for orders under $800 (US customs de minimis threshold).
