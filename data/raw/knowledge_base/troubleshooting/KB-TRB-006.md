# Audio Device Troubleshooting

## Overview

This guide covers common issues with headphones, earbuds, soundbars, and Bluetooth speakers purchased from Acme Store.

## No Sound from Headphones/Earbuds

1. Check that headphones are fully plugged in (wired) or paired (wireless).
2. Check volume on both the device and the headphone.
3. Test with a different audio source to isolate the issue.
4. Try different audio format settings (e.g., disable spatial audio on iPhone if causing issues).

For wireless:
- See KB-TRB-004 for Bluetooth connectivity.
- Check battery level of earbuds.

## One Side Not Working (Wired)

1. Plug in fully and wiggle the cable near the plug — intermittent connection may indicate a damaged cable.
2. Test in a different audio device.
3. Check balance settings on the device: Settings > Accessibility > Audio/Visual > Balance (iOS).

If one side is dead with no cable movement, this may be a driver failure — covered under warranty within 1 year.

## Microphone Not Working

1. Check app permissions — microphone access must be granted.
2. Select the correct input device: System settings > Sound > Input.
3. Check that the mic is not muted on the headset (many headsets have physical mic mute buttons).
4. Blow gently into the mic to check if it is blocked.

## Distorted or Crackling Sound

1. Lower the volume — distortion at high volumes may indicate the driver is being over-driven.
2. Clean the speaker grille/mesh.
3. Test with a different audio file — the source file may be corrupted.
4. Check for interference (especially on wireless devices — move away from Wi-Fi router).
5. Update firmware via the companion app.

## Soundbar Not Syncing to TV

1. Try HDMI ARC or optical cable connection instead of Bluetooth.
2. Ensure the TV's audio output is set to "External Speakers" or "ARC".
3. Disable the TV's internal speakers.
4. Perform a factory reset on the soundbar and re-setup.
