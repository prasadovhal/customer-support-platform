# Smart Home Device Setup and Troubleshooting

## Overview

Acme Store sells a variety of smart home devices including smart speakers, plugs, thermostats, and lighting. This article covers setup and common troubleshooting.

## Initial Setup — Smart Devices

1. Download the device's companion app (e.g., Acme Smart Home).
2. Create or log in to your account.
3. Ensure your phone is connected to the Wi-Fi network you want the device to use (2.4 GHz recommended for most smart home devices).
4. Follow the in-app setup wizard.
5. Most devices require pressing a setup button to enter pairing mode.

## Device Not Found During Setup

1. Ensure the device is powered on and in setup mode (LED indicator may flash rapidly).
2. Your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi network.
3. Disable VPN on your phone during setup.
4. Move the device closer to the Wi-Fi router.
5. Use 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi — most smart home devices do not support 5 GHz.

## Device Goes Offline Frequently

1. Check Wi-Fi signal strength at the device's location.
2. Add a Wi-Fi extender or mesh network node near the device.
3. Ensure the device firmware is up to date.
4. Restart the device and your Wi-Fi router.
5. Check if the device has a static IP assignment to avoid DHCP lease conflicts.

## Alexa / Google Home Integration

To connect Acme Store smart devices with voice assistants:

1. Open the Alexa or Google Home app.
2. Go to **More** > **Skills & Games** (Alexa) or **+ Add** > **Set up device** (Google Home).
3. Search for "Acme Smart Home" and link your account.
4. Devices should appear automatically.

If devices do not appear, unlink and relink the Acme Smart Home skill/service.

## Smart Thermostat Calibration

If the thermostat reads incorrect temperatures:

1. Check placement — thermostats should not be near heat sources, drafts, or direct sunlight.
2. Calibrate: Settings > Calibration > adjust offset in +/- degrees.
3. Allow 24 hours for the thermostat to learn your schedule.
