# Mobile App Troubleshooting

## Overview

The Acme Store mobile app is available for iOS (iOS 14+) and Android (Android 9+). This article covers common app issues and how to resolve them.

## App Crashes on Launch

**Steps to resolve:**

1. Force close the app and reopen.
2. Restart your device.
3. Check for app updates in the App Store or Google Play.
4. Clear the app cache (Android: Settings > Apps > Acme Store > Storage > Clear Cache).
5. If still crashing, uninstall and reinstall the app.

If the issue persists after reinstall, it may be a compatibility issue. Contact support with your device model and OS version.

## App Is Slow or Unresponsive

1. Check your internet connection (switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data).
2. Close other apps running in the background.
3. Clear the app cache.
4. Ensure you have at least 500 MB of free storage.

## Cannot Log In to the App

See KB-TRB-002 for general login troubleshooting. App-specific steps:

1. Force close and reopen the app.
2. Try logging out and back in.
3. Check if the website works — if both fail, there may be a service outage.

## Push Notifications Not Working

1. Check notification permissions: Device Settings > Apps > Acme Store > Notifications.
2. Ensure notifications are enabled in the app: App Settings > Notifications.
3. Uninstall and reinstall the app to reset notification registration.
4. Some phones with battery optimization may suppress notifications — disable optimization for the Acme Store app.

## Orders Not Loading in App

1. Pull down to refresh the orders list.
2. Check internet connectivity.
3. Log out and back in.
4. Clear app data (note: this will require you to log in again).

## Checkout Not Working in App

1. Ensure your saved payment method is up to date.
2. Try checking out from a web browser instead.
3. If a promo code is not applying, verify it is valid and enter it manually.

## App Update Issues

If an app update fails to install:

- iOS: Check available storage; ensure iOS is up to date.
- Android: Clear Play Store cache; retry the update.

## Reporting App Bugs

Found a bug? Report it through the app: **Menu** > **Help** > **Report a Problem**. Include a description and a screenshot if possible.
