# General Product Troubleshooting Guide

**Version 2.0 — Effective January 1, 2026**

## Overview

Before contacting support, try the troubleshooting steps in this guide. Many common issues can be resolved without needing to send a device in for repair.

## Step 1: Basic Checks

For any product issue, start here:

- Ensure the device is charged or connected to power.
- Check that all cables are firmly connected.
- Restart the device (power off completely, wait 30 seconds, power back on).
- Check for firmware or software updates.

## Device Won't Turn On

1. Connect to power source and wait 15 minutes.
2. Try a different charging cable or adapter.
3. Hold the power button for 10–15 seconds (force restart).
4. If still not turning on, contact support — this may indicate a hardware failure.

## Connectivity Issues (WiFi, Bluetooth)

1. Toggle the connection off and on again.
2. Forget the network/device and reconnect.
3. Restart your router or Bluetooth source device.
4. Check if other devices can connect — to isolate whether the issue is the device or network.
5. Reset network settings (last resort — check device manual for steps).

## Performance Issues (Slow, Freezing)

1. Close unused applications.
2. Restart the device.
3. Check available storage — low storage can cause slowness. Free up space.
4. Check for software updates.
5. If issue persists after update, consider a factory reset (back up data first).

## Display Issues

- **Dead pixels:** If more than 5 pixels are non-functional in a cluster, this may qualify as a warranty defect. Contact support.
- **Screen flickering:** Update display drivers/firmware. If persistent, hardware issue — contact support.
- **Brightness issues:** Check auto-brightness settings.

## Audio Issues

1. Check volume level and mute status.
2. Try headphones to isolate speaker vs. device issue.
3. Check audio settings in device menu.
4. Restart the device.

## When to Contact Support

Contact support if:

- The device does not turn on after all steps above.
- There is visible physical damage (cracks, burns).
- The issue appeared immediately out of the box (DOA — see KB-RET-003).
- The device is within warranty and has a recurring hardware issue.

## Before Sending for Repair

If your device needs to be sent in:

1. Back up all personal data.
2. Remove personal accounts (Google, Apple ID, etc.).
3. Factory reset the device if possible.
4. Note the serial number for tracking.
