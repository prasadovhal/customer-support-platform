# Software and Firmware Updates

## Overview

Keeping your devices' software and firmware up to date ensures optimal performance, security patches, and access to new features.

## Why Updates Matter

- **Security patches**: Fix vulnerabilities that could be exploited.
- **Bug fixes**: Resolve known stability and performance issues.
- **New features**: Unlock capabilities added after product launch.
- **Battery optimization**: Many updates improve power management.

## Updating Smartphone Software

### iPhone (iOS)

1. Connect to Wi-Fi and plug in to charge.
2. Go to **Settings** > **General** > **Software Update**.
3. Tap **Download and Install**.

### Android

1. Go to **Settings** > **Software Update** (or **System** > **System Update**).
2. Tap **Check for Updates** and follow prompts.

## Updating Laptop Software

### Windows Update

1. Go to **Settings** > **Windows Update** > **Check for Updates**.
2. Install all available updates.
3. Restart when prompted.

### macOS Update

1. Go to **System Settings** > **General** > **Software Update**.
2. Click **Update Now**.

## Updating Firmware (IoT Devices, Routers, Smart Devices)

Firmware updates are typically done through the companion app:

1. Open the device's companion app.
2. Go to **Device Settings** > **Firmware** or **About**.
3. Click **Check for Update** or enable auto-update.

Some devices update automatically when connected to Wi-Fi overnight.

## Update Fails or Device Stuck

If an update fails:

1. Ensure stable internet connection and sufficient battery (50%+).
2. Free up storage space (at least 5 GB recommended for OS updates).
3. Restart the device and retry.
4. For corrupted updates: perform a factory reset and update from clean state.

**Do not unplug or turn off the device during an update** — this can cause firmware corruption.
