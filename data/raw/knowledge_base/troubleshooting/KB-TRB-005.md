# Battery and Charging Problems

## Overview

This article addresses common battery and charging issues for laptops, smartphones, tablets, and wearables purchased from Acme Store.

## Device Not Charging

1. Check that the charger is properly connected to both the device and the power outlet.
2. Try a different power outlet.
3. Inspect the charging cable for damage — frayed or bent cables are a common cause.
4. Clean the charging port with a dry, soft brush or compressed air.
5. Try a different, compatible charger (same wattage and connector type).

If charging still fails, the issue may be a faulty charging port — covered under warranty if within 1 year.

## Battery Draining Rapidly

1. Check battery-intensive apps: Settings > Battery > Battery Usage (iOS/Android).
2. Reduce screen brightness.
3. Disable location services and background app refresh.
4. Enable battery saver / low power mode.
5. Update the operating system — battery drain bugs are often patched in updates.

If the battery degrades below 80% of original capacity within 1 year, it is covered under warranty.

## Device Charges Very Slowly

1. Use the original charger — third-party chargers often deliver less wattage.
2. Ensure fast charging is enabled (if supported): Settings > Battery > Fast Charging.
3. Close all apps while charging.
4. Remove device case — some cases trap heat and slow charging.

## Battery Swelling

A swollen battery is a safety hazard. **Stop using the device immediately.**

1. Do not try to puncture or remove the battery yourself.
2. Contact support for a warranty replacement.
3. Do not dispose of a swollen battery in regular trash — use an electronics recycling center.

Swollen batteries are covered under warranty if within the warranty period.

## Laptop Battery Not Detected

If your laptop shows "No battery detected" or "Plugged in, not charging":

1. Perform a battery reset: shut down, remove battery (if detachable), hold power for 30 seconds, reinsert battery and restart.
2. Update the battery drivers (Windows: Device Manager > Batteries).
3. Run Windows Battery Diagnostics.

For non-detachable batteries, contact support.
