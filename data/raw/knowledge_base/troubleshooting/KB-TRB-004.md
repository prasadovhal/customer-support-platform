# Bluetooth Pairing and Connectivity Issues

## Overview

This article covers troubleshooting for Bluetooth pairing failures and connection drops for headphones, speakers, wearables, and other Bluetooth-enabled devices purchased from Acme Store.

## Device Won't Enter Pairing Mode

1. Check the device's battery level — low battery may prevent pairing.
2. Hold the power/pairing button for 5-7 seconds until you see the pairing indicator (usually a flashing LED or audible beep).
3. Check the user manual — pairing activation may differ by model.

## Device Not Found by Phone/Computer

1. Ensure the device is in pairing mode (see above).
2. Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your phone/computer.
3. Move devices within 3 feet of each other for initial pairing.
4. Remove the device from your phone's saved Bluetooth list and re-pair.

On iPhone: Settings > Bluetooth > (i) next to device > Forget This Device
On Android: Settings > Connections > Bluetooth > (gear icon) > Unpair

## Device Pairs but No Audio

1. Check that the Bluetooth device is selected as the audio output:
   - Windows: System Tray > Sound > Select Output Device
   - Mac: System Preferences > Sound > Output
   - iPhone: Control Center > Audio Output (AirPlay icon)
2. Check volume on both the device and the connected speaker/headphone.
3. Disconnect and reconnect the Bluetooth device.

## Frequent Disconnections

1. Ensure no physical obstacles or interference between devices.
2. Move away from Wi-Fi routers — 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi can interfere with Bluetooth.
3. Update the device firmware via the companion app.
4. Reset the device to factory settings and re-pair.

## Bluetooth Range Issues

Most consumer Bluetooth devices operate at up to 10 meters (33 feet) in open space. Walls and interference reduce this range. For better range:

- Use Class 1 Bluetooth devices (rated up to 100 meters).
- Minimize obstacles between the device and phone.
