# Standard Warranty Policy

**Version 2.0 — Effective January 1, 2026**

## Overview

All products sold by Acme Store come with a manufacturer warranty. This document describes warranty coverage, claim processes, and what is and is not covered.

## Warranty Periods by Category

| Product Category | Standard Warranty |
|----------------|-----------------|
| Laptops | 24 months |
| Smartphones | 12 months |
| Tablets | 12 months |
| Electronics (general) | 12 months |
| Home Appliances | 24 months |
| Wearables | 12 months |
| Audio | 12 months |
| Networking | 12 months |
| Gaming | 12 months |
| Accessories | 6 months |

Warranty periods begin from the **confirmed delivery date**.

## What Is Covered

The standard warranty covers:

- Manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship
- Component failures under normal use conditions
- Dead-on-arrival (DOA) products
- Display defects (e.g., dead pixels exceeding manufacturer threshold)
- Battery failure within specified cycles/months

## What Is NOT Covered

The warranty does not cover:

- Physical damage caused by customer (drops, impacts, liquid damage)
- Damage from unauthorized modification or repair
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect functionality
- Normal wear and tear
- Damage from operating outside specified environmental conditions
- Software issues not caused by hardware defects
- Consumable parts (e.g., batteries after normal lifecycle)

## How to File a Warranty Claim

1. Contact Acme Store support with:
   - Order number
   - Product serial number (usually on the device or packaging)
   - Description of the defect
   - Photos or video of the issue (recommended)

2. Support will review the claim and may request the device for inspection.

3. Approved claims result in:
   - Repair (typically 7–14 business days)
   - Replacement with same or equivalent model
   - Refund (in limited circumstances)

## Warranty Claim Resolution Options

| Preference | Availability |
|-----------|-------------|
| Repair | Standard for all products |
| Replacement | Same model, subject to availability |
| Refund | Only if product discontinued or repair not feasible |

## Proof of Purchase

Warranty claims require proof of purchase. Your Acme Store order number serves as proof of purchase and is linked to the delivery date (warranty start date).

If you received the product as a gift, the gift recipient can register the warranty using the order number provided by the purchaser.
