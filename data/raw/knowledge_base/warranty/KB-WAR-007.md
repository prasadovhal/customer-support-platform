# International Warranty Coverage

## Overview

Warranty coverage for Acme Store products depends on where the product was purchased and where the warranty claim is filed.

## US-Purchased Products Used Abroad

Products purchased from Acme Store US (acmestore.com) carry US-specific warranty terms. If you travel or relocate internationally:

- **Warranty claims** must be submitted through Acme Store US (online or by shipping the product back to a US service center).
- International on-site service is not available unless you have an enterprise service contract.
- Shipping costs for international warranty service are covered by Acme Store for defective items.

## Region-Locked Products

Some products are region-locked (particularly smartphones and networking equipment). Using a US-purchased device in a region with incompatible frequencies or standards does not qualify for warranty coverage on region-specific failures.

| Product Type | Region Concern |
|-------------|----------------|
| Smartphones | LTE/5G band compatibility |
| Wi-Fi routers | Regulatory frequency bands |
| Power adapters | Voltage compatibility (110V vs 220V) |

Acme Store is not liable for damage caused by using products outside their intended regional specifications.

## International Customers Purchasing from Acme Store US

Acme Store does ship internationally. However:

- Warranty service for international customers requires shipping the product to the US.
- Return shipping for warranty claims from outside the US is at the customer's expense.
- Acme Store covers return shipping of repaired/replaced items back to the customer.

## Enterprise International Coverage

Enterprise customers with a global service contract receive international on-site warranty service. Contact your enterprise account manager for details.
