# Accidental Damage Protection

## Overview

Accidental Damage Protection (ADP) is available as part of **AcmeCare Plus** plans. It covers damage resulting from unintentional accidents that are not covered under standard warranties.

## What Counts as Accidental Damage

Covered incidents:

- **Drop damage**: Physical damage from a single accidental drop.
- **Liquid spills**: Damage from liquid contact (not submersion, unless device is rated waterproof).
- **Cracked screens**: Cracked or shattered displays from impact.
- **Key surges**: Electrical damage from power fluctuations.

Not covered:

- Damage from intentional abuse or negligence.
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect functionality.
- Loss or theft of the device.
- Damage occurring during an unauthorized repair.
- Damage from fire, earthquake, or other natural disasters (covered by homeowner's insurance, not ADP).

## Claim Limits

| Coverage Period | Max Claims | Max Claim Value |
|----------------|------------|-----------------|
| Per year | 2 claims | Up to product purchase price |

If the cost of repair exceeds the product's current market value, a replacement may be provided.

## How to File an Accidental Damage Claim

1. Report the incident within 30 days of occurrence.
2. Go to **Account** > **AcmeCare** > **File Accidental Damage Claim**.
3. Describe the incident and upload photos of the damage.
4. A technician assesses the claim within 2 business days.
5. Approved claims are repaired or replaced at no charge.

## Deductible

A deductible applies per ADP claim:

| Product Value | Deductible per Claim |
|---------------|---------------------|
| Under $200 | $25 |
| $200–$500 | $49 |
| $500–$1,000 | $99 |
| Over $1,000 | $149 |
