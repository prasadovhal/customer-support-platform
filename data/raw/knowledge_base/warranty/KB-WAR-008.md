# Repair vs. Replacement Decision Process

## Overview

When a warranty claim is approved, Acme Store determines whether to repair or replace the product based on the type of defect, repair cost, and product availability.

## Decision Criteria

| Factor | Repair | Replace |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Repair cost vs. product value | < 50% of product value | >= 50% of product value |
| Part availability | Parts available | Parts discontinued |
| Repair time | < 5 business days | > 10 business days |
| Repeat failure | First occurrence | Second or more |
| AcmeCare Plus claim | Possible | Preferred for accidental damage |

## Repair Process

When repair is selected:

1. The customer ships the product to the designated service center.
2. A prepaid shipping label is provided.
3. The service center diagnoses and repairs the defect.
4. Typical repair time: 5–10 business days after receipt.
5. The repaired product is shipped back with a 90-day repair warranty.

**If the repair fails within 90 days**, a direct replacement is provided.

## Replacement Process

When replacement is selected:

- A **refurbished unit of the same model** (or the closest current equivalent) is shipped.
- The replacement carries the remaining warranty period of the original product.
- If the same model is discontinued, a comparable current model is offered.

## Customer Preference

Customers may state a preference for repair or replacement, which Acme Store will consider but is not obligated to fulfill. The final decision is made by the warranty team based on the criteria above.

## Total Loss

If a product is declared a total loss (beyond economic repair and replacement not feasible):

- A refund equal to the product's current market value is offered.
- If under AcmeCare, the full purchase price may be refunded (less deductible).
