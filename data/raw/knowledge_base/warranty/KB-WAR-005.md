# Commercial Use Warranty Limitations

## Overview

Products purchased through Acme Store's consumer channel are warranted for personal, non-commercial use. Using consumer products in commercial settings may limit or void warranty coverage.

## Standard Consumer Warranty

The 1-year standard warranty (KB-WAR-001) is designed for:

- Personal home use
- Light home-office use (occasional professional tasks)

## Commercial Use Definition

"Commercial use" is defined as deploying products in environments where:

- The product is used 8+ hours per day continuously.
- The product is operated by multiple different users (not just the owner).
- The product is used in a professional/production environment (e.g., retail kiosk, call center workstation).
- The product generates revenue directly.

## Impact on Warranty

| Use Type | Standard Warranty | AcmeCare Plus |
|----------|------------------|----------------|
| Personal use | Full coverage | Full coverage |
| Home office (light) | Full coverage | Full coverage |
| Commercial (moderate) | 50% coverage | 75% coverage |
| Heavy commercial | Warranty voided | Not applicable |

## Business Warranty Solutions

For commercial deployments, Acme Store recommends:

- **Business-grade products** (see KB-PRD-001) with commercial warranties.
- **Enterprise hardware service contracts** (contact enterprise sales).
- **On-site service plans** for high-uptime environments.

Business-grade products include commercial warranties that cover multi-user, high-intensity deployments.

## How to Disclose Commercial Use

When filing a warranty claim, you will be asked to confirm the use environment. Misrepresenting use type may result in claim denial and account review.
