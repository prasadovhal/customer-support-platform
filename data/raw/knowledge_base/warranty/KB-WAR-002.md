# Extended Warranty Options

**Version 2.0 — Effective January 1, 2026**

## Overview

Acme Store offers extended warranty plans (AcmeCare Plans) that provide coverage beyond the standard manufacturer warranty period.

## Available Plans

| Plan | Duration | Coverage | Price |
|------|----------|----------|-------|
| AcmeCare Basic | +12 months | Manufacturer defects only | 8% of product price |
| AcmeCare Plus | +24 months | Defects + accidental damage | 15% of product price |
| AcmeCare Pro | +36 months | Full coverage including repairs | 22% of product price |

All plans begin immediately after the standard warranty expires.

## Accidental Damage Coverage (AcmeCare Plus and Pro)

AcmeCare Plus and Pro plans cover:

- Accidental drops and impacts
- Liquid spills (up to two incidents per year)
- Screen damage

Coverage limits:
- Up to 2 claims per year
- Claims are subject to a $50 deductible (AcmeCare Plus) or $25 deductible (AcmeCare Pro)

## Purchase Window

Extended warranty plans must be purchased within **30 days of product delivery**.

After 30 days, extended warranty plans are not available for purchase.

## How to Purchase

Extended warranty plans can be added:
- At checkout when purchasing the product
- Through Account > Order History > Add Protection within 30 days of delivery

## How to File an Extended Warranty Claim

The process is the same as standard warranty claims — contact support with your order number and plan details.

## Transferability

AcmeCare plans are tied to the product serial number and are **transferable** if the product is sold or gifted.

## Cancellation

AcmeCare plans can be cancelled within 30 days for a full refund. After 30 days, a pro-rated refund is available for unused coverage.
