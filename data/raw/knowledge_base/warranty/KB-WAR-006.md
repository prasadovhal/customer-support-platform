# Warranty Transfer and Resale

## Overview

Acme Store warranties are tied to the product, not the original purchaser, unless otherwise specified. This means remaining warranty coverage can transfer to a new owner if the product is sold or gifted.

## Standard Warranty Transfer

The standard 1-year manufacturer warranty transfers automatically with the product. New owners benefit from the remaining coverage period.

**Example**: If a laptop was purchased on January 1, 2026, and sold on July 1, 2026, the new owner has warranty coverage until January 1, 2027.

## AcmeCare Extended Warranty Transfer

AcmeCare plans can be transferred:

1. Contact support at warranty@acmestore.com with:
   - Product serial number
   - Original order number
   - New owner's name and email
2. The plan is transferred within 2 business days.
3. The new owner receives a confirmation email with claim instructions.

**Note**: AcmeCare plans can only be transferred once.

## Proof of Purchase for Warranty Transfers

The new owner should retain:

- A copy of the original purchase receipt (or order number).
- The AcmeCare plan transfer confirmation email.
- The product serial number.

These documents are required when filing warranty claims.

## Warranty Voiding Conditions

Warranty (including transferred warranties) is voided if:

- The product has been opened and repaired by an unauthorized service center.
- The serial number label has been removed or altered.
- The product shows signs of water damage not covered by ADP.
- The product was involved in an insurance claim.

## Second-Hand Market Disclaimer

Products purchased from unauthorized resellers, third-party marketplaces, or non-Acme-Store channels may not carry a valid Acme Store warranty. Acme Store recommends purchasing directly or from authorized retailers.
