# Extended Warranty Plans

## Overview

Acme Store offers extended warranty plans (called **AcmeCare**) that extend coverage beyond the standard manufacturer's warranty.

## AcmeCare Plans

| Plan | Duration | Coverage | Price |
|------|----------|----------|-------|
| AcmeCare 1-Year | 1 additional year | Manufacturing defects | 8% of product price |
| AcmeCare 2-Year | 2 additional years | Manufacturing defects | 14% of product price |
| AcmeCare Plus 2-Year | 2 additional years | Defects + accidental damage | 20% of product price |
| AcmeCare Plus 3-Year | 3 additional years | Defects + accidental damage | 27% of product price |

## What AcmeCare Covers

### Standard AcmeCare (Defects Only)

- Mechanical and electrical failures
- Component failures under normal use
- Power surges
- Battery degradation below 80% of original capacity (after year 1)

### AcmeCare Plus (Defects + Accidental Damage)

Everything in Standard, plus:

- Accidental drops and spills
- Cracked screens
- Liquid damage

**Note:** Intentional damage, loss, or theft is not covered under any AcmeCare plan.

## How to Purchase AcmeCare

- **At checkout**: Add AcmeCare when purchasing the product.
- **Within 30 days of purchase**: Go to **Account** > **My Products** > select item > **Add AcmeCare**.
- After 30 days from purchase, AcmeCare cannot be added to an existing product.

## Filing a Claim Under AcmeCare

1. Go to **Account** > **AcmeCare** > select your plan.
2. Click **File a Claim** and describe the issue.
3. A technician appointment is scheduled within 3 business days.
4. Eligible repairs are covered at no cost (or replacement provided).

## AcmeCare Plan Transferability

AcmeCare plans are tied to the product serial number, not the account. If you sell the product, the remaining AcmeCare coverage transfers to the new owner. Contact support to update ownership records.
