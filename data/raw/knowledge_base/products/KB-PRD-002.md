# Smartphones — Buying Guide and Product Overview

## Overview

Acme Store carries a wide range of smartphones from leading manufacturers. This guide helps you choose the right device.

## Smartphone Categories

| Category | Use Case | Price Range |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Entry-level | Calling, basic apps, messaging | $99–$299 |
| Mid-range | Social media, casual photography, gaming | $300–$599 |
| Flagship | Pro-grade camera, max performance | $600–$1,299 |
| Foldable | Dual screens, multitasking | $900–$1,999 |

## Key Specifications

- **Operating System**: iOS (Apple) or Android (Google, Samsung, etc.).
- **Display**: Size (5.5"–7"), resolution, refresh rate (60–165 Hz), material (AMOLED vs LCD).
- **Camera**: Megapixels matter less than sensor size and software. Look for optical zoom, night mode, and video quality.
- **Battery**: 4,000–6,000 mAh; fast charging and wireless charging support.
- **5G Support**: Essential for future-proofing; check your carrier's 5G band compatibility.
- **Storage**: 128 GB minimum; many flagships do not include expandable storage.

## Network Compatibility

| Carrier Type | Compatible Bands |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Verizon | 5G mmWave + Sub-6 GHz |
| AT&T | 5G Sub-6 GHz |
| T-Mobile | 5G Sub-6 GHz + mmWave |

**Important**: Phones purchased unlocked may not support all bands for every carrier. Verify before purchasing.

## Unlocked vs. Carrier-Locked Phones

All phones sold by Acme Store are **unlocked** and compatible with any carrier's SIM (subject to band compatibility). Carrier-specific phones from manufacturers can be found on carrier websites.

## Trade-In Program

Acme Store accepts trade-ins of qualifying smartphones. Trade-in value is applied as a discount on your new phone purchase. Check trade-in value at acmestore.com/trade-in.
