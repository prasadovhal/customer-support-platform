# Wearables — Smartwatches and Fitness Trackers

## Overview

Acme Store carries a range of wearable devices including smartwatches, fitness bands, and health monitors.

## Product Categories

### Smartwatches

Full-featured watches with app support, notifications, and health tracking.

| Type | Best For | Price Range |
|------|----------|-------------|
| Apple Watch | iPhone users, health focus | $249–$799 |
| Galaxy Watch | Android/Samsung users | $199–$699 |
| Google Pixel Watch | Android, Wear OS | $299–$449 |
| Luxury smartwatches | Fashion + smart features | $400–$2,000+ |

### Fitness Trackers

Simpler devices focused on health and activity tracking.

- Band form factor (wristband style).
- Key metrics: Steps, heart rate, sleep tracking, calories.
- Price range: $39–$199.

### Health Monitors

Specialized devices:

- Smart rings: Discreet health tracking (sleep, HRV, temperature).
- ECG monitors: Medical-grade heart rate recording.
- Blood pressure watches: Requires calibration with cuff.

## Key Features to Evaluate

- **Battery life**: 1-7 days (smartwatches); 7-30 days (fitness trackers).
- **Health sensors**: Heart rate, SpO2, ECG, body temperature, stress.
- **GPS**: Built-in (on-device) vs. connected (uses phone GPS).
- **Water resistance**: ATM rating — 3 ATM for splash, 5 ATM for swimming, 10 ATM for diving.
- **Display**: AMOLED (vibrant, power hungry) vs. always-on e-ink (battery efficient).

## Compatibility

- Apple Watch: **Requires iPhone** (iPhone 8 or newer, iOS 16+).
- Galaxy Watch: Best with Samsung Android; limited features on iPhone.
- Fitness trackers: Usually cross-platform via companion app.
