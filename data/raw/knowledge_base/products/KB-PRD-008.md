# Gaming Peripherals — Buying Guide

## Overview

Acme Store carries a full range of gaming peripherals including monitors, keyboards, mice, headsets, controllers, and gaming chairs.

## Gaming Monitors

| Spec | Budget | Mid-Range | High-End |
|------|--------|-----------|---------|
| Resolution | 1080p (FHD) | 1440p (QHD) | 4K (UHD) |
| Refresh Rate | 60-144 Hz | 144-240 Hz | 240-360 Hz |
| Response Time | 4 ms | 1 ms | <1 ms |
| Panel Type | TN | IPS | IPS/OLED |
| Price | $150–$299 | $300–$599 | $600–$1,999 |

For competitive gaming, prioritize refresh rate (144 Hz+). For content creation, prioritize resolution and color accuracy.

## Gaming Keyboards

- **Membrane keyboards**: Quiet, lower cost ($20–$80).
- **Mechanical keyboards**: Tactile/clicky feedback, customizable switches ($50–$300+).
  - Red switches: Linear, quiet, fast.
  - Blue switches: Clicky, tactile.
  - Brown switches: Tactile bump, quieter than blue.
- **Wireless mechanical keyboards**: Freedom from cables ($80–$250).

## Gaming Mice

Key metrics:

- **DPI**: 400–16,000 DPI — higher is not always better; 800–1,600 DPI is most common in gaming.
- **Polling rate**: 125–8,000 Hz — higher reduces input lag.
- **Grip style**: Palm, claw, fingertip — choose by hand size and preferred grip.

## Gaming Headsets

- Look for: Surround sound (7.1), noise-cancelling mic, comfort for long sessions.
- Wired headsets offer the lowest latency.
- Wireless headsets offer 15–30 hours of battery life.

## Controllers

- Platform-specific controllers (PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo) work on their native platform.
- Xbox controllers work natively on Windows PC via USB or Bluetooth.
- Third-party controllers available for cross-platform use.
