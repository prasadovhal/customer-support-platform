# Laptops — Buying Guide and Product Overview

## Overview

Acme Store carries a curated selection of laptops across all performance tiers, from budget education devices to enterprise-grade workstations.

## Laptop Categories at Acme Store

| Category | Use Case | Price Range |
|----------|----------|-------------|

## Key Specifications to Consider

- **Processor (CPU)**: Intel Core i5/i7/i9 or AMD Ryzen 5/7/9 for Windows; Apple M-series for Mac.
- **RAM**: 8 GB (minimum), 16 GB (recommended), 32+ GB (professional).
- **Storage**: 256 GB (light use), 512 GB (standard), 1 TB+ (media/gaming).
- **Display**: Resolution (1080p vs 1440p vs 4K), refresh rate (60 Hz vs 120+ Hz), panel type (IPS vs OLED).
- **Battery**: Measured in watt-hours (Wh); 60 Wh+ recommended for all-day use.
- **GPU**: Integrated (standard tasks), dedicated (gaming, video editing, ML).

## Business and Enterprise Laptops

Business-grade laptops include features not found in consumer models:

- TPM 2.0 chip for enterprise security.
- MIL-SPEC durability ratings.
- vPro management capabilities.
- Longer warranty options.
- ISV certifications (for professional software).

## Compatibility Notes

- macOS laptops (Apple) are not compatible with Windows software natively.
- Thunderbolt 4 docks provide single-cable docking for supported laptops.
- Check USB-C charging compatibility for charging accessories.
