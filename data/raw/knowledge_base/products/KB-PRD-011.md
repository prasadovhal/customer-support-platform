# Product Compatibility Guide

## Overview

Compatibility issues are among the most common reasons for returns. This guide helps you verify product compatibility before purchase.

## How to Find Compatible Products

### Method 1: Acme Store Compatibility Filter

On any product listing:

1. Click **Check Compatibility**.
2. Enter your main device's brand and model.
3. Compatible accessories and add-ons are highlighted.

### Method 2: Model Number Search

1. Find your device's model number (Settings > About, or printed on the device label).
2. Search for your model number in the Acme Store search bar.
3. Results are filtered to show compatible products.

## Common Compatibility Scenarios

### Laptop Accessories

| Accessory | What to Check |
|-----------|---------------|
| Docking station | Thunderbolt vs USB-C version, OS compatibility |
| RAM upgrade | Speed (DDR4/5), form factor (SO-DIMM), maximum supported |
| Storage upgrade | NVMe vs SATA, M.2 slot size (2230/2242/2280) |
| Charger | Wattage (min. required), connector type |

### Smartphone Accessories

| Accessory | What to Check |
|-----------|---------------|
| Case | Exact phone model (e.g., iPhone 16 Pro vs. 16 Pro Max) |
| Screen protector | Exact model + curved screen consideration |
| Wireless charger | Qi standard, wattage |
| Cable | Connector type (USB-C, Lightning) |

### Smart Home Compatibility

Most smart home devices use Zigbee, Z-Wave, Wi-Fi, or Matter protocol:

- **Matter**: Cross-platform standard — works with Apple HomeKit, Google Home, Amazon Alexa.
- **Zigbee/Z-Wave**: Requires a compatible hub.
- **Wi-Fi only**: Direct connection, no hub needed.

Check device listings for ecosystem compatibility logos.

## When In Doubt

Contact Acme Store support with:

- Your main device brand, model, and operating system version.
- The product you are considering.

Support can verify compatibility and suggest alternatives.
