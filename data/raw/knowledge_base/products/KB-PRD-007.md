# Home Appliances — Buying Guide

## Overview

Acme Store carries major home appliances including washing machines, refrigerators, dishwashers, air conditioners, and small kitchen appliances.

## Energy Efficiency Ratings

Energy Star certified appliances use 10–50% less energy than standard models. Look for the Energy Star label when comparing products.

Energy rating labels show estimated annual electricity cost — a useful long-term cost comparison.

## Installation

Large appliances may require professional installation:

- Washing machines: Water connection, leveling.
- Dryers: Venting (gas line for gas dryers requires licensed technician).
- Dishwashers: Under-counter installation.
- Air conditioners: Window or through-the-wall installation.

White Glove Delivery (KB-SHP-007) is available for large appliances and includes basic placement.

## Appliance Compatibility

- Gas vs. electric: Verify your home has the appropriate utility connection.
- Voltage: Most US appliances require 110V or 220V — check local requirements.
- Door swing: Measure refrigerator door clearance before purchase.

## Return Policy

Large appliances follow a **15-day return window** and require White Glove pickup for returns. See KB-SHP-007.
