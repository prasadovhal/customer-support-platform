# Product Care and Maintenance Guide

## Overview

Proper care extends the life of your electronics and reduces the likelihood of needing warranty claims or replacements. This guide covers general care tips by product category.

## Laptops and Computers

- **Cleaning**: Use a microfiber cloth with 70% isopropyl alcohol to clean screen and keyboard. Never spray liquid directly on the device.
- **Ventilation**: Keep vents clear; use on hard surfaces, not soft bedding (blocks airflow).
- **Battery health**: Avoid 100% charge for prolonged periods; charge between 20–80% for optimal battery longevity.
- **Storage**: Use a padded laptop bag. Avoid stacking heavy items on a closed laptop.
- **Software hygiene**: Keep OS and drivers updated; run antivirus scans periodically.

## Smartphones and Tablets

- **Screen protection**: Apply a tempered glass screen protector immediately.
- **Charging**: Use the included or certified charger. Avoid charging overnight when battery is already full.
- **Heat**: Keep out of direct sunlight; extreme heat degrades batteries.
- **Water**: Even IP-rated devices can fail if water enters ports. Rinse with fresh water after saltwater exposure; do not charge while wet.
- **Storage**: Do not expose to magnetic fields or strong RF sources.

## Audio Products

- **Headphones**: Store in a case when not in use. Wrap cables loosely (figure-eight style) to avoid kinks.
- **Earbuds**: Clean tips with a dry cotton swab. Never use liquid near driver mesh.
- **Speakers**: Keep away from water sources. Use covers when not in use.

## Wearables

- **Straps**: Rinse silicone straps with fresh water after sweaty workouts; dry before wearing.
- **Charging**: Use the supplied magnetic charger; avoid third-party chargers.
- **Screen**: Apply protective film; avoid scratching against hard surfaces.

## General Electronics

- **ESD safety**: Avoid touching circuit boards without grounding yourself (important for DIY upgrades).
- **Temperature**: Store electronics in 50–85°F (10–30°C). Avoid leaving in hot cars.
- **Dust**: Use compressed air to clear dust from vents annually.

## When Maintenance Isn't Enough

If a product fails despite proper care, warranty coverage (KB-WAR-001) or AcmeCare (KB-WAR-003) may apply.
