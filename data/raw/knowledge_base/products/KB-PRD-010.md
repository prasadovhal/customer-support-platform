# Refurbished Products — Overview and Policy

## What Are Refurbished Products?

Refurbished products are pre-owned devices that have been returned, inspected, repaired (if necessary), cleaned, and certified to work like new. Acme Store sells **Certified Refurbished** products at discounted prices.

## Sources of Refurbished Products

Refurbished products come from:

1. **Customer returns**: Products returned within the return window with no defects.
2. **Open-box items**: Products opened for display or demonstration.
3. **Manufacturer refurbishments**: Devices repaired and recertified by the manufacturer.

## Grading System

| Grade | Condition | Price vs New |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| Grade A (Like New) | No visible scratches or marks | 10–20% off |
| Grade B (Good) | Minor cosmetic marks, fully functional | 20–35% off |
| Grade C (Acceptable) | Visible wear, fully functional | 35–50% off |

All product listings clearly state the grade and include photos of the actual device.

## What Is Included

All Certified Refurbished products include:

- The core device (all functions tested and verified).
- A new or refurbished compatible charger/cable.
- 90-day limited warranty (see KB-WAR-001 and KB-RET-010).

Original boxes are not always included — this is noted in the listing.

## What Is Not Included

- Original accessories beyond the charger (unless specifically stated).
- Original software licenses (device is factory reset; OS reinstall may be needed).
- Extended warranty coverage (AcmeCare plans are available separately — see KB-WAR-003).

## Refurbished Product Return Policy

Refurbished products have a **15-day return window** for standard customers. See KB-RET-010 for full details.

## Why Buy Refurbished?

- Save 10–50% vs. new.
- Environmentally sustainable (extends device life).
- Same performance as new (all components tested).
- 90-day warranty provides peace of mind.
