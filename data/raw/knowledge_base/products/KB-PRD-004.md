# Audio Products — Headphones, Earbuds, and Speakers

## Overview

Acme Store carries a comprehensive range of audio products, from budget earbuds to high-end audiophile headphones and home theater systems.

## Headphones

### Over-Ear Headphones

- Best for: Home listening, studio monitoring, long sessions.
- Key feature: Largest drivers for the best sound quality and bass.
- Popular options: Noise-cancelling (ANC) for commuters; open-back for audiophiles.
- Price range: $39–$699.

### On-Ear Headphones

- Best for: Portability with larger sound than earbuds.
- Price range: $29–$299.

## Earbuds and In-Ear Monitors

### True Wireless (TWS) Earbuds

- No cable between buds; stored in a charging case.
- Key specs: Battery life per charge, ANC, water resistance (IPX rating).
- Price range: $29–$399.

### Wired In-Ear Earphones

- No battery needed; reliable connection.
- Price range: $9–$299.

## Speakers

### Portable Bluetooth Speakers

- Battery-powered; for outdoor/travel use.
- Key specs: Battery life, water resistance (IP67+), loudness.
- Price range: $29–$299.

### Home Speakers / Soundbars

- Designed for living rooms; wired or wireless.
- Soundbars: Connect to TV for cinematic audio.
- Price range: $49 (soundbar) to $2,999 (hi-fi system).

## Key Audio Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| ANC | Active Noise Cancellation — reduces background noise electronically |
| IPX4/IP67 | Splash/water resistance rating |
| Codec | Audio compression standard (AAC, aptX, LDAC) — affects wireless quality |
| Hz response | Frequency range — wider is better for bass and treble |
| Impedance | Ohms — higher impedance headphones need amplifiers |

## Hygiene Note

Earbuds and in-ear earphones are hygiene products and are non-returnable once opened. See KB-RET-006.
