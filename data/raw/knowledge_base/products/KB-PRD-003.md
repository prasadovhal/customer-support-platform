# Tablets — Buying Guide and Product Overview

## Overview

Tablets bridge the gap between smartphones and laptops. Acme Store carries tablets for every use case, from children's education devices to professional creative tools.

## Tablet Categories

| Category | Best For | Price Range |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Kids/Education | Learning apps, parental controls | $99–$249 |
| Standard Android | Entertainment, casual productivity | $150–$499 |
| iPad (standard) | Creative work, Apple ecosystem | $329–$599 |
| iPad Pro | Professional design, video editing | $799–$1,499 |
| Android Pro | Productivity, stylus support | $499–$1,099 |
| 2-in-1 Tablets | Laptop replacement | $599–$1,299 |

## Key Specifications

- **Display Size**: 7"–13" — larger is better for content creation; smaller for portability.
- **Processor**: A-series (Apple) or Snapdragon/MediaTek (Android); affects performance and battery life.
- **RAM**: 4 GB (standard), 8–12 GB (pro/multitasking).
- **Storage**: 64–2,048 GB; check if expandable storage (microSD) is supported.
- **Cellular vs. Wi-Fi Only**: Cellular tablets work with a SIM card for on-the-go connectivity.
- **Stylus Support**: Required for digital art, note-taking; check compatibility (e.g., Apple Pencil, S-Pen).

## Accessories Compatibility

Keyboards, cases, and styluses are often model-specific. Always verify accessory compatibility:

- iPad Pro (M4) requires specific Apple Magic Keyboard and Apple Pencil Pro.
- Samsung Galaxy Tab S series supports S-Pen; not all Android tablets do.

## Use Case Recommendations

| User Type | Recommended Device |

| Student | Standard iPad or mid-range Android |
| Professional artist | iPad Pro + Apple Pencil |
| Business road warrior | 2-in-1 Android tablet with keyboard |
