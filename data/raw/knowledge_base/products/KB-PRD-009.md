# Accessories — Cables, Cases, and Adapters

## Overview

Acme Store carries thousands of accessories for your devices, including cables, cases, chargers, adapters, and protective gear.

## Cables and Charging

### USB-C Cables

Not all USB-C cables are equal. Key differences:

| Spec | Basic USB-C | USB 3.2 Gen 2 | Thunderbolt 4 |
|------|------------|---------------|---------------|
| Data Speed | 480 Mbps | 10 Gbps | 40 Gbps |
| Power Delivery | Up to 60W | Up to 100W | Up to 240W |
| Video | No | Yes (DP Alt Mode) | Yes (2x4K) |
| Price | $5–$10 | $15–$30 | $25–$50 |

Always check your device's supported standard before purchasing a cable.

### Charging Adapters

- **Wall chargers**: Look for USB Power Delivery (USB-PD) and wattage matching your device's fast charge requirement.
- **GaN chargers**: Gallium Nitride technology — smaller, cooler, more efficient than traditional chargers.
- **Wireless chargers**: Qi standard is universal; look for 15W+ for fast wireless charging.

## Phone Cases

| Case Type | Protection Level | Use Case |
|-----------|-----------------|----------|
| Slim case | Low | Minimal bulk, moderate protection |
| Folio/wallet case | Medium | Cards, phone together |
| Rugged case | High | Construction, outdoor use |
| OtterBox/UAG | Very High | Maximum protection |
| Clear case | Low | Show the phone design |

Always verify case model compatibility — cases are phone-model specific.

## Screen Protectors

- **Tempered glass**: Best protection, minimal screen feel change.
- **PET film**: Thinner, less protective, easier to apply.
- **Privacy filters**: Block side-view for sensitive environments.

## Compatibility Guide

When purchasing accessories:

1. Note your device's model number (found in Settings > About).
2. Filter Acme Store products by model compatibility.
3. Look for "Works with [device]" certification badges.
