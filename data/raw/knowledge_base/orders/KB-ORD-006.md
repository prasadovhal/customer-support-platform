# Price Match Policy

## Overview

Acme Store offers a price match guarantee: if you find the same product at a lower price from a qualifying retailer, we will match it.

## Eligibility Requirements

### Product Requirements

- Identical product: same brand, model, SKU, and condition (new, not refurbished).
- Must be in stock at the competing retailer at the time of the request.
- Full product listings only — bundle deals, limited-time flash sales, and auction prices are excluded.

### Retailer Requirements

Eligible competing retailers:

- Amazon (for items sold and shipped by Amazon, not third-party sellers)
- Best Buy
- Walmart.com
- Target.com
- B&H Photo Video
- Newegg

Not eligible:

- Marketplace sellers (eBay, third-party Amazon sellers)
- Outlet or clearance stores
- Wholesale clubs (Costco, Sam's Club)
- Overseas retailers

### Timing Requirements

- Price match requests must be submitted **within 14 days of purchase**.
- The lower price must be currently active (not expired).

## How to Request a Price Match

1. Contact support via chat or email.
2. Provide: your order number, the competing retailer URL, and the listed price.
3. Support verifies the price within 2 business hours.
4. If approved, the difference is refunded to your original payment method.

## Acme Store Price Drops

If Acme Store reduces the price of an item you purchased within 14 days, you are also eligible for a price adjustment. No external retailer comparison is needed — just contact support with your order number.
