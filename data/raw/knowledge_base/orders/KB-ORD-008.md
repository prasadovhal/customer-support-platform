# Order History and Record Keeping

## Accessing Order History

Your complete order history is available in your Acme Store account:

1. Log in to acmestore.com.
2. Go to **Account** > **Order History**.
3. Filter by date range, product category, or order status.

Each order shows:

- Order number and date
- Item list with quantities and prices
- Order status (placed, processing, shipped, delivered, cancelled, returned)
- Tracking information (if shipped)
- Refund status (if applicable)

## Order Data Retention

Acme Store retains order records for:

| Record Type | Retention Period |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Order details | 7 years (tax compliance) |
| Shipping records | 3 years |
| Payment records | 7 years (PCI DSS requirement) |
| Return records | 3 years |
| Conversation logs | 2 years |

After retention periods, data is anonymized or deleted per our Privacy Policy.

## Downloading Order Records

For each order, you can download:

- **PDF receipt** — for personal expense records.
- **CSV export** — for integration with accounting software.
- **Formal invoice** — for business expense reporting (Business/Enterprise accounts).

To export all orders: Go to **Account** > **Order History** > **Export All** (CSV format, filtered by date).

## Order History for Returns and Warranty

Your order history is required for:

- Initiating returns (order confirmation date determines return window).
- Filing warranty claims (proof of purchase date).
- Requesting price matches.

If you do not have an Acme Store account (guest checkout), retain your order confirmation email as proof of purchase.

## Guest Orders

Guest orders are accessible for 1 year via the order lookup tool at acmestore.com/track using:

- Order number
- Email address used at checkout

After 1 year, guest orders are purged from the lookup system. Creating an account retroactively links guest orders if the same email is used.
