# Order Modification Policy

**Version 2.0 — Effective January 1, 2026**

## Overview

Once an order is placed, modification options are limited and time-sensitive. This document explains what can and cannot be changed after an order is submitted.

## What Can Be Modified

The following changes may be possible **before the order enters processing**:

| Modification | Eligible? | Conditions |
|-------------|-----------|-----------|
| Delivery address | Possibly | Only before shipment label is generated |
| Shipping method upgrade | Yes | Subject to availability and price difference |
| Quantity reduction | Possibly | Contact support immediately |
| Item color/size variant | Possibly | Only if alternate variant is in stock |
| Remove item (multi-item order) | Possibly | Contact support immediately |

## What Cannot Be Modified

- Changing to a completely different product (cancel and reorder instead)
- Adding new items to an existing order
- Changing payment method after order is placed
- Modifying quantity after order enters processing

## Time Sensitivity

Order modification requests must be submitted **within 1 hour** of order placement for the highest chance of success. After 1 hour, the order may already be in warehouse processing.

For urgent modifications, use live chat or phone — email may not be responded to in time.

## Address Change Requests

Address changes after shipment are not possible through Acme Store. For in-transit address changes, you must contact the carrier directly using the tracking number. Carrier address change services may have fees and availability varies.

Acme Store is not responsible for delivery failures due to address changes made directly with the carrier.

## Upgrade Shipping After Order

Shipping method upgrades (e.g., from Standard to Express) may be available before the order ships. Contact support with your order number. If an upgrade is applied, you will be charged the difference.

## Modification Not Possible?

If your desired modification is not possible, the recommended approach is:
1. Cancel the original order (if still within cancellation window — see KB-ORD-001).
2. Place a new order with the correct details.
