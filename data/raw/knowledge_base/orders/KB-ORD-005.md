# Pre-Order Policy

## What Is a Pre-Order?

A pre-order allows customers to reserve a product before its official release date. Pre-orders are available for upcoming product launches, limited-edition items, and highly anticipated restocks.

## How Pre-Orders Work

1. Select a pre-order eligible product and click **Pre-Order Now**.
2. Complete checkout — your payment method is authorized but **not charged** at this time.
3. Your card is charged on the **ship date** (typically 1-2 days before the release date).
4. You receive a confirmation email with the expected ship date.

## Pre-Order Guarantees

Acme Store guarantees:

- **Price protection**: If the item's price drops before ship date, you pay the lower price.
- **Priority fulfillment**: Pre-orders ship before walk-in or post-launch orders.
- **Release date delivery**: If you select express shipping, we guarantee delivery on or by the release date (in eligible areas).

## Cancelling a Pre-Order

You may cancel a pre-order at any time before the charge date:

1. Go to **Account** > **Orders** > find the pre-order.
2. Click **Cancel Pre-Order**.
3. The authorization hold on your card is released within 3-5 business days.

Once the charge has been processed (within 2 days of ship date), cancellation follows the standard order cancellation policy (see KB-ORD-001).

## If a Pre-Order Is Delayed

If the release date is delayed:

- You receive an email notification with the new expected ship date.
- You may cancel the pre-order at no cost if the delay exceeds 30 days.

## If a Pre-Order Is Unavailable (Cancelled by Acme Store)

If Acme Store cancels a pre-order due to supply issues:

- Full refund is issued (or authorization released if not yet charged).
- You receive priority access when the product becomes available.
