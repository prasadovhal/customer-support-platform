# Order Limits and Purchase Restrictions

## Overview

To ensure fair product availability and prevent reseller stockpiling, Acme Store applies purchase limits on certain products and account types.

## Per-Order Quantity Limits

Standard limits per order:

| Product Category | Max Quantity Per Order |
|-----------------|------------------------|
| Smartphones | 2 |
| Laptops | 2 |
| Gaming consoles | 1 |
| Limited-edition products | 1 |
| Standard accessories | 10 |
| Home appliances | 2 |

Business and Enterprise accounts may request higher limits by contacting their account manager.

## Per-Account Monthly Limits

In addition to per-order limits, accounts are subject to monthly purchase limits for certain categories:

| Product Category | Monthly Limit (Standard) | Monthly Limit (Premium) |
|-----------------|--------------------------|------------------------|
| Smartphones | 3 | 5 |
| Gaming consoles | 2 | 3 |
| Limited-edition | 2 | 4 |

## Restricted Products

Some products require age or identity verification:

- Products containing restricted chemicals require age verification (18+).
- Professional/commercial-grade equipment may require a business license.

## Order Limit Notifications

If you exceed the allowed quantity:

- Excess items are automatically removed from your cart.
- A notification explains the limit applied.
- You may split future purchases across billing periods.

## Why These Limits Exist

Limits protect availability for all customers and prevent automated purchase bots from buying out stock during high-demand launches. Fair access ensures every customer has a chance to purchase popular products.
