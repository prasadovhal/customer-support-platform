# Bulk and Wholesale Orders

## Overview

Acme Store offers volume discounts and streamlined ordering for business customers purchasing 10 or more units of the same product.

## Volume Discount Tiers

| Quantity | Discount |
|----------|----------|
| 10–24 units | 5% off list price |
| 25–49 units | 10% off list price |
| 50–99 units | 15% off list price |
| 100–499 units | 20% off list price |
| 500+ units | Custom pricing (contact Sales) |

Discounts apply per product SKU and cannot be combined with other promotions.

## How to Place a Bulk Order

### Option 1: Standard Checkout (10-49 units)

For orders under 50 units, place them directly through the website. Volume discounts are automatically applied at checkout when the quantity threshold is met.

### Option 2: Bulk Order Form (50+ units)

For 50 or more units:

1. Go to **Business** > **Bulk Orders**.
2. Fill out the Bulk Order Request Form with item SKUs and quantities.
3. An account manager contacts you within 1 business day with a custom quote.
4. Approve the quote to generate a bulk order.

## Bulk Order Lead Times

| Order Size | Estimated Lead Time |
|------------|---------------------|
| 10–49 units | 3-5 business days |
| 50–99 units | 5-10 business days |
| 100+ units | 10-15 business days |
| Custom/configured | Per quote |

## Payment Options for Bulk Orders

- Credit card (immediate charge)
- Purchase Order / Net-30 (Business accounts only — see KB-PAY-007)
- Wire transfer

## Bulk Order Returns

- Bulk orders follow standard return policies per product category.
- Returns of more than 25 units require prior authorization from the enterprise support team.
- Restocking fees of 5% may apply for bulk returns without defect.
