# Order Confirmation, Receipts, and Documentation

## Order Confirmation Email

After placing an order, you receive an order confirmation email within 5 minutes. This email contains:

- Order number (format: ORD-XXXXX)
- List of ordered items with quantities and prices
- Applied discounts and coupon codes
- Tax and shipping cost breakdown
- Total amount charged
- Estimated delivery date
- Link to track your order

If you do not receive a confirmation email:

1. Check your spam folder.
2. Verify you used the correct email address.
3. Go to **Account** > **Order History** to confirm the order was placed.
4. Contact support if the order does not appear in order history.

## Order Number Format

Order numbers at Acme Store follow the format **ORD-XXXXX** (e.g., ORD-01234). Always have your order number ready when contacting support.

## Dispatch Confirmation

When your order ships, you receive a dispatch confirmation email containing:

- Carrier name (UPS, FedEx, USPS)
- Tracking number (clickable link)
- Estimated delivery date (updated)
- Package dimensions and weight

## Delivery Confirmation

After delivery, a delivery confirmation email is sent with:

- Confirmation of delivery date and time
- Signature capture (if applicable)
- Prompt to rate your delivery experience

## Order Documentation for Business Accounts

Business and Enterprise customers can download:

- **Itemized invoices** for expense reporting.
- **Packing lists** for inventory management.
- **Customs documentation** for international shipments.

Access these from **Account** > **Order History** > select order > **Documents**.
