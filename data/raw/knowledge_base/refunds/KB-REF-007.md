# Refund Disputes and Escalation

## Overview

If you disagree with a refund decision — whether a full refund was denied, a partial refund was issued, or a refund is taking longer than expected — this article explains how to escalate.

## Step 1: Review Your Refund Status

Before escalating, check:

1. Log into your account and go to **Orders** > **Returns & Refunds**.
2. Review the refund status and any notes.
3. Verify the expected timeline based on your refund method (see KB-REF-002).

Most refund delays resolve within 10 business days of return receipt.

## Step 2: Contact Customer Support

If the refund status is "Error" or "On Hold", contact support:

- **Email:** refunds@acmestore.com
- **Chat:** Available 8 AM – 8 PM EST on acmestore.com
- **Phone:** 1-800-ACME-123 (Mon–Fri, 9 AM – 6 PM EST)

Provide:
- Order number
- Return tracking number
- Refund reference ID (if available)

## Step 3: Formal Escalation

If the standard support team cannot resolve your dispute within 5 business days, request escalation to a **Senior Refund Specialist**.

To escalate:
- Reply to your support ticket with "ESCALATE - REFUND DISPUTE"
- Include all prior correspondence

A Senior Specialist will contact you within 2 business days.

## Step 4: Chargeback (Last Resort)

If Acme Store has not resolved a legitimate refund claim within 30 days of the return being confirmed received, you may dispute the charge with your bank (chargeback). Note:

- Chargebacks may result in temporary account suspension pending investigation.
- Acme Store cooperates with all legitimate bank investigations.
- If the chargeback is found invalid, a dispute fee may apply.

## Common Dispute Scenarios

| Scenario | Resolution Path |
|----------|-----------------|
| Refund not received after 10 days | Contact support with return tracking number |
| Partial refund disputed | Email photos of returned item's condition |
| Refund to wrong payment method | Contact support to redirect |
| Different refund amount than expected | Request itemized refund breakdown |
