# Partial Refunds

**Version 2.0 — Effective January 1, 2026**

## Overview

A partial refund is a refund for less than the full purchase amount. This document explains when partial refunds are issued and how they are calculated.

## Scenarios for Partial Refunds

### 1. Partial Return (Multi-Item Order)

When a customer returns only some items from a multi-item order, a partial refund is issued for the returned items only.

**Calculation:** Refund = (Unit price × quantity returned) − any applicable restocking fee

### 2. Opened Electronics Restocking Fee

When an opened electronics item is returned for a non-defect reason, a restocking fee of up to 15% may be applied.

**Example:** Laptop purchased at $999. Returned opened for buyer's remorse. Restocking fee = $149.85. Refund = $849.15.

Note: No restocking fee applies to defective or damaged items.

### 3. Price Adjustment Refund

If an item purchased at regular price goes on sale within **7 days** of purchase, customers may request a one-time price adjustment refund.

**Calculation:** Refund = (Original price − Sale price) × quantity purchased

Price adjustment requests must be submitted within 7 days of the price change. Maximum one adjustment per item per order.

### 4. Partially Damaged Order

If a multi-item order arrives with some items damaged and others intact, a partial refund or replacement is offered for the damaged items only.

## How to Request a Partial Refund

1. Log into your account and navigate to **Order History**.
2. Select the order and click **Report an Issue** or **Initiate Return**.
3. Specify the item(s) and the reason.
4. Support will calculate and confirm the refund amount before processing.

## Partial Refund Timeline

Partial refunds follow the same timeline as full refunds — see KB-REF-001 for details.

## Disputes

If you disagree with the partial refund amount, contact billing@acmestore.example with your order number and the expected refund calculation.
