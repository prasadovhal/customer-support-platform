# Gift Card and Store Credit Refunds

## Overview

This article explains how refunds are handled when the original payment was made using an Acme Store gift card, store credit, or voucher.

## Refunds to Gift Cards

If an order was paid with a gift card:

- The refund is returned to the **original gift card**.
- If the original gift card is no longer available, a **new gift card** is issued for the same amount.
- Gift card refunds are processed within 24–48 hours.

## Refunds to Store Credit

If an order was paid using store credit:

- The refund is returned to your store credit balance.
- Store credit has **no expiration date**.
- Store credit can be used for any future purchase on Acmestore.com.

## Mixed Payment Refunds

If an order was paid using a combination of payment methods (e.g., $50 store credit + $30 credit card):

- The refund is applied proportionally.
- $50 is returned to store credit.
- $30 is returned to the credit card.

## Gift Card Purchases: No Cash Value

Gift cards and store credits cannot be redeemed for cash. If an order refund would result in a store credit balance greater than $500, the excess over $500 may be converted to a check refund upon request.

## Using Store Credit

Store credit is applied at checkout:

1. At checkout, select "Use Store Credit".
2. The available balance is shown.
3. Enter the amount to apply (or apply all).
4. Remaining balance is charged to your default payment method.

## Expiration and Validity

| Credit Type | Expiration |
|-------------|-----------|
| Store Credit | Never expires |
| Promotional Credit | 90 days from issuance |
| Compensation Credit | 12 months from issuance |
| Gift Cards | Never expires |
