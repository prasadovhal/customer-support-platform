# Partial Refunds Policy

## Overview

In certain situations, Acme Store issues partial refunds rather than full refunds. This article explains when partial refunds apply and how they are calculated.

## When Partial Refunds Are Issued

### 1. Incomplete Returns

If a product is returned without all included accessories (chargers, cables, manuals, etc.), the refund is reduced by the value of missing components.

| Missing Item | Typical Deduction |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Power adapter | $15–$40 |
| Carrying case | $10–$25 |
| Original packaging | $5–$15 |
| Bundled accessories | Retail value of accessory |

### 2. Returned Items with Damage

Items returned with damage beyond normal wear (scratches, cracks, liquid damage) may receive a reduced refund based on the assessed impact on resale value.

| Damage Level | Refund Percentage |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Light marks | 90% of purchase price |
| Moderate wear | 75% of purchase price |
| Significant damage | 50% of purchase price |
| Severe damage | Return rejected |

### 3. Used or Opened Consumables

If consumable items (batteries, ink, etc.) are partially used and returned, the refund is prorated based on the remaining quantity.

### 4. Partial Shipment Returns

If an order contained multiple items and only some are returned, the refund covers only the returned items plus applicable return shipping.

### 5. Price Adjustment Refunds

When a price adjustment is granted (see KB-PAY-001), only the difference between the original price and the new price is refunded.

## Notification of Partial Refund

Customers are notified by email with:

- Itemized deduction breakdown
- Final refund amount
- Reason for deduction
- Instructions to dispute if disagreed

## Disputing a Partial Refund

If you believe a deduction was made in error:

1. Reply to the refund notification email within 7 days.
2. Include photos of the returned item's condition.
3. A senior agent will review and respond within 3 business days.
