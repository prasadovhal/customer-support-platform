# Refund Policy and Timelines

**Version 2.0 — Effective January 1, 2026**

## Overview

This document describes when refunds are issued, how they are processed, and typical timelines for customers to receive their money back.

## When Refunds Are Issued

Refunds are issued in the following circumstances:

| Scenario | Refund Type |
|----------|------------|
| Item returned within policy window | Full refund |
| Damaged/defective item returned | Full refund |
| Order cancelled before shipment | Full refund |
| Order cancelled after shipment (if returned) | Full refund minus return shipping if applicable |
| Partial return (some items from order) | Partial refund for returned items |
| Price adjustment (item went on sale) | Partial refund for price difference |

## Refund Methods

Refunds are returned to the original payment method:

| Payment Method | Refund Method |
|----------------|--------------|
| Credit card | Credit to the same card |
| Debit card | Credit to the same card |
| PayPal | Returned to PayPal balance |
| Bank transfer | Returned to originating bank account |
| Store credit | Returned as store credit |

Acme Store does not issue refunds to a different payment method than the one used for the original purchase, except in cases where the original payment method no longer exists (e.g., closed card).

## Processing Timelines

| Step | Typical Timeline |
|------|----------------|
| Return received and inspected | 1–3 business days after receipt |
| Refund initiated by Acme Store | 1–2 business days after inspection |
| Credit card refund visible | 5–10 business days (bank-dependent) |
| PayPal refund visible | 3–5 business days |
| Bank transfer refund | 5–10 business days |

**Total typical timeline:** 7–15 business days from the date the item is received by Acme Store.

## Tracking Your Refund

You will receive an email notification when your refund is initiated. You can also check refund status in your account under Order History.

If you have not received your refund within the stated timeline, contact your bank before contacting Acme Store — some banks take longer to process credits.

## Refund Disputes

If you believe a refund is incorrect in amount or has not been received after the stated timeline, contact support at billing@acmestore.example with your order number and the date the refund was initiated.

## Authorization Holds

Acme Store may place a temporary authorization hold when an order is placed. This is not a charge — it is released automatically if the order is cancelled before fulfillment.
