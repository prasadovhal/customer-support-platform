# Refunds for Cancelled Orders

## Overview

When an order is cancelled — whether by the customer or by Acme Store — the refund process depends on the cancellation timing and payment method.

## Customer-Initiated Cancellations

### Before Processing (Status: Placed)

If you cancel an order while it is still in "placed" or "confirmed" status:

- **Full refund** is issued automatically.
- No shipping charges apply.
- Refund timeline: 1-3 business days to credit card; immediate to store credit.

### After Processing Begins (Status: Processing)

If the order is already in processing:

- Cancellation may not be possible.
- Contact support immediately — we may be able to intercept.
- If intercepted successfully: full refund.
- If already dispatched: treated as a return after delivery.

### After Shipping (Status: Shipped/Out for Delivery)

Order cannot be cancelled once shipped. Options:

1. Refuse delivery — the package is returned to Acme Store automatically.
2. Accept delivery and initiate a return (see KB-RET-001).

## Acme Store-Initiated Cancellations

Acme Store may cancel orders due to:

- Out-of-stock items after order placement
- Payment verification failure
- Suspected fraudulent activity
- Shipping restrictions (address not serviceable)

In these cases, a **full refund** is always issued with an explanatory email.

## High-Value Order Cancellations

Orders over $1,000 that are cancelled after processing begins require supervisor approval for refund processing. This is a high-risk action subject to additional identity verification.

## Refund Timelines for Cancellations

| Cancellation Stage | Refund Timeline |
|-------------------|-----------------|
| Before processing | 1-3 business days |
| After processing (intercepted) | 3-5 business days |
| After return from refused delivery | 5-10 business days |
