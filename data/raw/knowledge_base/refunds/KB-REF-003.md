# Cancelled Order Refunds

**Version 2.0 — Effective January 1, 2026**

## Overview

This document explains the refund process specifically for cancelled orders, including timelines and conditions based on the stage of order processing at the time of cancellation.

## Cancellation Before Shipment

If an order is cancelled **before it has shipped**, a full refund is issued automatically.

- No restocking fee applies.
- Refund is initiated within **1 business day** of cancellation confirmation.
- Authorization holds (if applicable) are released immediately.

See KB-ORD-001 for order cancellation eligibility and process.

## Cancellation After Shipment

If an order has already shipped, it cannot be cancelled in transit. The customer must:

1. Refuse the delivery when it arrives (item is returned to sender), OR
2. Accept delivery and then initiate a return through the standard return process.

Once the item is received back by Acme Store, a full refund is processed. No restocking fee applies for carrier-returned or refused packages.

**Note:** Refused delivery packages take 5–15 business days to be returned to Acme Store's warehouse. The refund is initiated after receipt.

## Partial Cancellation (Multi-Item Orders)

For orders with multiple items, individual items can sometimes be cancelled if they have not yet been packed. Contact support immediately — partial cancellations are not guaranteed once order processing has begun.

## Refund for Cancelled Pre-Orders

Pre-order cancellations follow the same policy: full refund if cancelled before the item ships.

If a pre-order is cancelled by Acme Store (e.g., product discontinued), a full refund is issued automatically without requiring customer action.

## Timeline Summary

| Cancellation Stage | Refund Processing |
|--------------------|------------------|
| Before shipment | 1–2 business days |
| After delivery (return process) | 7–15 business days after item received |
| Refused delivery | 10–20 business days (transit + processing) |

For all refund timelines, additional bank processing time applies. See KB-REF-001.
