# Same-Day and Next-Day Delivery

## Overview

Acme Store offers same-day and next-day delivery options for eligible orders in select metropolitan areas.

## Same-Day Delivery

### Availability

Same-day delivery is available in the following metro areas:

- New York City (Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens)
- Los Angeles (LA County)
- Chicago (City proper)
- San Francisco Bay Area
- Seattle (King County)
- Miami (Miami-Dade County)

Check availability at checkout by entering your delivery ZIP code.

### Requirements

- Order must be placed by **12:00 PM local time** for same-day delivery.
- Item must be **in stock at a local fulfillment center**.
- Same-day delivery is not available for large/heavy items (see KB-SHP-007).
- Minimum order value: $25.

### Cost

| Membership | Same-Day Cost |
|------------|---------------|
| Standard   | $12.99        |
| Premium    | $4.99         |
| Business   | $8.99         |
| Enterprise | Per agreement |

## Next-Day Delivery

### Availability

Next-day delivery is available across the continental US (excluding remote areas).

### Requirements

- Order placed before **6:00 PM** (customer's local time).
- Item in stock at a regional warehouse.

### Cost

| Membership | Next-Day Cost |
|------------|---------------|
| Standard   | $7.99         |
| Premium    | Free (up to 4/month) |
| Business   | $4.99         |
| Enterprise | Per agreement |

## Delivery Windows

For same-day delivery, customers can select a 2-hour delivery window:

- Morning: 8 AM – 12 PM
- Afternoon: 12 PM – 4 PM
- Evening: 4 PM – 8 PM

## Failed Delivery

If same-day delivery fails due to the driver's inability to reach you:

- A re-delivery attempt is made the next business day.
- The same-day fee is refunded as store credit.
