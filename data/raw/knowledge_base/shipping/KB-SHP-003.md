# Lost Package Policy

**Version 2.0 — Effective January 1, 2026**

## Overview

If your tracked shipment shows as delivered but you have not received it, or if tracking has not updated for an extended period, this document explains how Acme Store investigates and resolves lost package claims.

## Definition: Lost Package

A package is considered potentially lost in one of two situations:

1. **Tracking shows "Delivered" but item not received** — The carrier's system records a delivery that the customer cannot locate.
2. **No tracking update for 10+ business days** (domestic) or **21+ business days** (international) — The shipment appears stuck or has no further movement.

## Step 1: Self-Service Checks

Before contacting support, please:

1. Check all delivery locations at your address (front door, mailbox, garage, side entrance).
2. Ask neighbors if they accepted the package on your behalf.
3. Check with your building reception, mailroom, or parcel locker if applicable.
4. Verify the delivery address on your order confirmation is correct.
5. Wait one additional business day — carriers occasionally mark packages as delivered slightly before actual delivery.

## Step 2: Contact Acme Store Support

If the package cannot be located after the above checks, contact support within **30 days of the reported delivery date** (or last tracking update for undelivered packages).

Provide:
- Order number
- Confirmation that you completed the self-service checks above
- Your current delivery address

## Step 3: Carrier Investigation

Acme Store will file a formal investigation with the carrier. The carrier investigation typically takes **3–7 business days**.

During the investigation period:
- Acme Store may contact you for additional information.
- The carrier may contact you directly.

## Resolution

After the investigation is complete:

| Outcome | Resolution |
|---------|-----------|
| Package located | Delivery rescheduled or redirected |
| Package confirmed lost | Full refund or replacement shipped |
| Carrier determines "delivered" (disputed) | Escalated to senior team for review |

In cases where the carrier confirms delivery but the customer disputes receipt, Acme Store will conduct a senior review. Resolution may take additional time.

## Claim Window

Lost package claims must be submitted within **30 days of the expected delivery date**. Claims submitted after 30 days may not be eligible for replacement or refund.
