# Large and Heavy Item Shipping

## Overview

Large appliances, furniture-class items, and products exceeding standard carrier limits require special shipping arrangements. This article explains how Acme Store handles oversized items.

## What Qualifies as a Large Item

Items are classified as large/heavy if they meet any of the following:

- Weight exceeds 70 lbs (32 kg)
- Any single dimension exceeds 60 inches
- Product category: washing machines, refrigerators, large TVs (65"+), treadmills

## Shipping Method: White Glove Delivery

Large items are shipped using **White Glove Delivery** service, which includes:

- Scheduled delivery appointment (typically 3-7 business days lead time)
- Two-person delivery team
- Delivery to a room of your choice (ground floor or elevator-accessible)
- Assembly of flat-pack or simple assembly items (where applicable)
- Removal of old appliances (upon request, subject to local regulations)

## Delivery Appointment Scheduling

After placing an order:

1. A logistics partner contacts you within 24 hours.
2. You choose from available time slots (typically 4-hour windows).
3. Confirmation email with appointment details is sent.

Premium members receive a $20 discount on white glove delivery fees.

## Delivery Requirements

Customers must ensure:

- Clear path from entrance to installation room.
- Adequate door clearance (minimum 30" wide).
- Elevator availability if above ground floor (no stair carries for items over 100 lbs).
- An adult (18+) present at delivery.
