# Holiday Shipping and Seasonal Cutoff Dates

## Overview

During peak seasons, shipping carriers experience higher volumes, which can delay deliveries. Acme Store recommends ordering early during the holiday period.

## 2026 Holiday Shipping Cutoff Dates

To receive orders by major holidays, place your order by these dates:

| Holiday | Holiday Date | Standard Cutoff | Express Cutoff | Same-Day Cutoff |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|----------------|-----------------|
| Independence Day | July 4 | June 28 | July 2 | July 3 |
| Thanksgiving | Nov 27 | Nov 20 | Nov 25 | Nov 26 |
| Christmas | Dec 25 | Dec 17 | Dec 22 | Dec 24 |
| New Year's Day | Jan 1 | Dec 26 | Dec 30 | Dec 31 |

**Note:** Cutoff dates are estimates. Carrier delays during peak season may affect delivery.

## Black Friday and Cyber Monday

During Black Friday (Nov 28) and Cyber Monday (Dec 1) weeks:

- Processing times may increase by 1-2 business days.
- Same-day delivery may be unavailable due to high volume.
- Express delivery is guaranteed only if selected and confirmed at checkout.

## Holiday Carrier Schedules

Most carriers do not operate on the following dates:

- Christmas Day (Dec 25)
- New Year's Day (Jan 1)
- Thanksgiving Day (Nov 27)
- Independence Day (Jul 4)

Orders placed before a carrier holiday are processed the next business day.

## Gift Wrapping and Scheduling

To guarantee gift arrival by Christmas:

- Order by December 17 (standard) or December 22 (express).
- Add gift wrapping at checkout.
- Include a personalized message to be printed on the gift note.

## Delay Compensation

If an order is delayed beyond the guaranteed delivery date due to Acme Store or carrier error (not weather-related), you may be eligible for a shipping fee refund. Weather delays are not eligible for compensation.
