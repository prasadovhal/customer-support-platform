# Shipping to PO Boxes and Military Addresses

## PO Box Shipping

Acme Store supports shipping to USPS PO Boxes for eligible items.

### Eligible for PO Box Delivery

- Small accessories (cables, cases, adapters)
- Books and documentation
- Small electronic accessories weighing under 2 lbs

### Not Eligible for PO Box Delivery

- Laptops, desktops, and large electronics
- Home appliances
- Items requiring signature confirmation
- Hazardous materials
- Items shipped via FedEx or UPS (these carriers do not deliver to PO Boxes)

### How to Ship to a PO Box

Enter the PO Box address at checkout. If the item is not eligible, you will be notified during checkout and asked to provide a physical address.

## Military and Diplomatic Addresses (APO/FPO/DPO)

Acme Store ships to APO (Army Post Office), FPO (Fleet Post Office), and DPO (Diplomatic Post Office) addresses via USPS only.

### Shipping to APO/FPO/DPO

- Enter the military address using standard format: Unit number, APO/FPO/DPO, State (AE/AP/AA), and ZIP code.
- Only USPS Priority Mail is available for military addresses.
- Some large or heavy items may not be eligible for military addresses.

### Delivery Times to Military Addresses

Delivery to military addresses can take 2-6 weeks depending on location and operational constraints. Tracking is available while the package is within the US postal network.

### Restrictions

- Electronics with lithium batteries may have restrictions depending on the destination country.
- Duty and import requirements at overseas bases follow SOFA (Status of Forces Agreement) guidelines.
