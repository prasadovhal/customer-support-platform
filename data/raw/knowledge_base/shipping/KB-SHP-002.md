# International Shipping Policy

**Version 2.0 — Effective January 1, 2026**

## Overview

Acme Store ships to select international destinations. This document covers international shipping timelines, costs, customs responsibilities, and restrictions.

## Supported Regions

Acme Store currently ships internationally to:

- Canada
- United Kingdom
- European Union (select countries)
- Australia
- Japan
- Selected other countries (check checkout for your country)

## International Shipping Timelines

| Destination | Standard | Express |
|-------------|----------|---------|
| Canada | 7–14 business days | 3–5 business days |
| UK/EU | 10–21 business days | 5–8 business days |
| Australia/Japan | 14–28 business days | 7–12 business days |

Timelines are estimates and do not include customs clearance time, which varies by country.

## Shipping Costs

International shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on destination, weight, and dimensions. No free shipping threshold applies to international orders.

## Customs, Duties, and Taxes

The customer is responsible for all import duties, taxes, and customs fees charged by the destination country. These charges are:

- Not included in the Acme Store order total
- Collected by the local customs authority or carrier at delivery
- Not refundable by Acme Store

Acme Store is required by law to accurately declare the value and description of items on customs documents.

## Restricted Items

Some products cannot be shipped internationally due to:

- Local regulations in the destination country
- Export control restrictions
- Carrier limitations

Electronics (particularly those with certain battery types) may have additional shipping restrictions for air freight. The checkout process will notify you of any restrictions for your selected destination.

## International Returns

International orders may be returned under the standard return policy, but the customer is responsible for international return shipping costs unless the item is defective or damaged. See KB-RET-003 for damaged/defective items.
