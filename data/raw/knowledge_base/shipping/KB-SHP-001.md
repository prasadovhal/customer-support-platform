# Domestic Shipping Policy

**Version 2.0 — Effective January 1, 2026**

## Overview

This document describes Acme Store's domestic shipping options, costs, and timelines for deliveries within the United States.

## Shipping Methods and Timelines

| Shipping Method | Estimated Delivery | Cost |
|----------------|-------------------|------|
| Standard | 5–10 business days | Free on orders over $50; $5.99 otherwise |
| Express | 2–4 business days | $12.99 |
| Next-Day | Next business day | $24.99 |

Estimated delivery times begin from the date of shipment (not order placement). Most orders are processed and shipped within 1–2 business days.

## Free Shipping Threshold

Standard shipping is free on orders totaling $50 or more after discounts and before tax.

Premium customers receive free express shipping on orders over $100. Enterprise customers have free shipping on all orders as part of their account benefits.

## Tracking

All shipped orders include a tracking number. You will receive:

1. A shipment confirmation email with the tracking number within 24 hours of dispatch.
2. The tracking number can be entered at the carrier's website or through your Acme Store account.

## Cut-off Times

Orders placed before **2:00 PM local time** on business days are typically processed and shipped the same day. Orders placed after the cut-off or on weekends/holidays are processed the next business day.

## Business Days

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding US federal holidays.

## Address Requirements

- Use complete addresses including apartment/suite numbers.
- PO Box delivery is supported for standard shipping only.
- Forwarding addresses are not guaranteed to receive shipments.

## Failed Delivery Attempts

If a delivery attempt fails:

1. The carrier will leave a delivery attempt notice.
2. You may arrange a redelivery via the carrier's website or contact our support team.
3. After 3 failed delivery attempts, packages are typically returned to sender. In such cases, Acme Store will contact you and reship at no additional charge (first occurrence).

## Signature Requirements

Orders over $500 may require a signature upon delivery. You will be notified in your shipping confirmation email if signature is required.
