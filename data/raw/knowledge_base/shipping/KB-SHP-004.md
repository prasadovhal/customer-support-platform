# Delayed Delivery Policy

**Version 2.0 — Effective January 1, 2026**

## Overview

Occasionally, shipments experience delays beyond the estimated delivery window. This document explains how Acme Store handles delivery delays and what customers can expect.

## What Counts as a Delay?

A shipment is considered delayed when:

- It has not been delivered by the end of the **estimated delivery window** shown at checkout.
- Tracking shows no update for **5+ business days** (domestic) or **10+ business days** (international).

Note: Estimated delivery dates are estimates, not guarantees, unless the order was placed with Next-Day shipping.

## Next-Day Shipping Guarantee

If you selected **Next-Day shipping** and your order was not delivered by the guaranteed date (next business day after dispatch), you are eligible for:

- A refund of the Next-Day shipping fee (automatically applied)
- Priority reshipping if the item is still in transit

## Standard and Express Delays

For Standard and Express shipping delays:

1. **Check tracking first** — carriers may show an updated delivery estimate.
2. If the delay exceeds 5 business days beyond the estimated delivery date, contact Acme Store support.
3. Support will contact the carrier to investigate and provide an updated ETA.

## Customer Options During a Delay

| Situation | Customer Options |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Still in transit, small delay | Wait for updated tracking ETA |
| Delay over 10 business days | Request carrier investigation OR cancel and refund |
| Item time-sensitive | Cancel order for refund (if not yet delivered) |
| Delay due to weather/force majeure | Refund of shipping upgrade fee only |

## Cancellation During Delay

If your order has not yet been delivered, you may request a cancellation and full refund at any time during a significant delay. Contact support with your order number.

Once delivered, the standard return policy applies.

## Holiday and Peak Periods

During peak periods (e.g., Black Friday, Cyber Monday, holiday season), carrier delays are more common. Acme Store lists estimated delay warnings on the checkout page during these periods. Shipping upgrade refunds are not issued for delays during communicated peak periods unless the Next-Day guarantee applies.

## Contact

For delay inquiries: support@acmestore.example or live chat in your account.
