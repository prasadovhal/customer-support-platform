# Account Suspension and Reinstatement

## Reasons for Account Suspension

An Acme Store account may be suspended for:

- **Payment issues**: Multiple failed payments or disputed chargebacks.
- **Policy violations**: Fraud attempts, return abuse, purchasing for resale in violation of terms.
- **Security concerns**: Suspected unauthorized access or account compromise.
- **Terms of Service violations**: Abusive communication, harassment of staff.
- **Age verification failure**: If account is used by a minor in a restricted category.

## Types of Suspension

| Type | Description | Duration |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Temporary hold | Placed pending investigation | 1-7 days |
| Restricted access | Can view but not order | Until issue resolved |
| Full suspension | No access | Until appeal resolved |
| Permanent ban | All access revoked | Permanent |

## Notification of Suspension

When an account is suspended:

1. An email is sent to the registered address explaining the reason.
2. Any active orders are not cancelled automatically (they continue to process unless separately flagged).
3. If the suspension is related to a security concern, a password reset may be forced.

## How to Request Reinstatement

For temporary or restricted suspensions:

1. Reply to the suspension notification email.
2. Provide the requested documentation (e.g., payment confirmation, identity verification).
3. The account team reviews within 3-5 business days.
4. Decision is communicated via email.

For permanent bans, reinstatement requests are reviewed only once. Appeal decisions are final.

## Account Suspension and Active Orders

If your account is suspended while orders are in transit:

- Orders already shipped are delivered as normal.
- Future orders cannot be placed.
- Return requests may be paused pending reinstatement.
