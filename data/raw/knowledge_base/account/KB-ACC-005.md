# Business Account Upgrade and Features

## Overview

A Business account unlocks volume pricing, invoice billing, multi-user management, and dedicated support for qualifying organizations.

## How to Upgrade to a Business Account

### Option 1: Apply Online

1. Log in to your account.
2. Go to **Account** > **Upgrade to Business**.
3. Fill out the business information form:
   - Company legal name
   - Business address
   - Tax ID / EIN
   - Annual purchase estimate
4. Submit the form. Review takes 1-2 business days.
5. Once approved, your account is upgraded and a dedicated account manager is assigned.

### Option 2: Contact Sales

For immediate assistance:

- Email: businesssales@acmestore.com
- Phone: 1-800-ACME-BIZ (Mon–Fri, 9 AM – 6 PM EST)

## Business Account Features

| Feature | Standard | Business | Enterprise |
|---------|----------|----------|------------|
| Volume discounts | No | Yes (5-20% off) | Yes (custom) |
| Net-30 billing | No | Yes | Yes |
| Multi-user management | No | Up to 5 users | Unlimited |
| Dedicated account manager | No | Shared | Dedicated |
| Priority support | No | Business hours | 24/7 |
| Custom reporting | No | Basic | Advanced |
| API access | No | Read-only | Full |

## Multi-User Management

Business accounts can add up to 5 users:

1. Go to **Account** > **Team Management** > **Add User**.
2. Enter the user's email and select their role (Purchaser, Viewer, Admin).
3. The user receives an invitation email.
4. Each user logs in with their own credentials.

## Upgrading from Business to Enterprise

When your annual spend exceeds $50,000 or you need advanced features (custom SLAs, API access, dedicated infrastructure):

- Contact enterprise sales to discuss an enterprise agreement.
- Enterprise agreements include custom pricing, SLAs, and dedicated support.
