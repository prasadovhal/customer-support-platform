# Communication Preferences and Notifications

## Overview

Acme Store sends notifications about orders, account activity, promotions, and security events. You can customize what you receive and how.

## Types of Notifications

### Transactional Notifications (Cannot be disabled)

These are essential for your account and orders:

- Order confirmation
- Shipment dispatch with tracking
- Delivery confirmation
- Return confirmation and refund status
- Password change and security alerts
- Fraud alerts

### Optional Notifications

You can enable or disable these:

| Notification | Default | Channel |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| Promotional emails | On | Email |
| Product recommendations | On | Email |
| Price drop alerts (wishlisted items) | Off | Email / Push |
| Order update SMS | Off | SMS |
| Restock alerts | Off | Email / Push |
| Newsletter | On | Email |

## Updating Your Preferences

1. Go to **Account** > **Notifications**.
2. Toggle each notification type on or off.
3. Choose your preferred channel (email, SMS, push notification).
4. Click **Save Preferences**.

You can also unsubscribe from marketing emails directly using the **Unsubscribe** link at the bottom of any marketing email.

## Push Notifications

Push notifications are sent through the Acme Store mobile app. To manage:

1. Open the Acme Store app.
2. Go to **Settings** > **Notifications**.
3. Enable or disable notification types.

Device-level notification permissions must also be granted in your phone's settings.

## SMS Notifications

To enable SMS:

1. Verify your phone number (see KB-ACC-002).
2. Go to **Account** > **Notifications** > **SMS**.
3. Enable your preferred SMS alerts.

Standard carrier message and data rates may apply.
