# Premium Membership Benefits

## Overview

Acme Store Premium is a paid membership tier offering exclusive benefits for frequent shoppers.

## Premium Membership Cost

- Monthly: $12.99/month
- Annual: $99.99/year (save ~36% vs monthly)

Free Premium trial: 30 days for new members (credit card required, no charge during trial).

## Benefits

### Shipping Benefits

- **Free standard shipping** on all orders (no minimum).
- **Free next-day delivery** on up to 4 orders per month.
- **Discounted same-day delivery** ($4.99 instead of $12.99).

### Return Benefits

- **Extended return window**: 45 days for standard products (vs 30 days), 30 days for electronics (vs 15 days).
- **Free return shipping** on all returns.
- **Priority return processing**: 1-2 business days (vs 3-5 standard).

### Savings Benefits

- **5% cashback** on all purchases, applied as store credit.
- **Early access** to sales and new product launches (24-48 hours before public).
- **Exclusive member prices** on select products.

### Support Benefits

- **Priority support queue**: Skip the wait in chat and phone.
- **Dedicated Premium support line**: Available 7 AM – 10 PM EST, 7 days a week.

## How to Subscribe

1. Go to **Account** > **Premium Membership**.
2. Choose monthly or annual plan.
3. Enter payment details.
4. Benefits activate immediately after payment.

## Cancelling Premium

Cancel anytime from **Account** > **Premium Membership** > **Cancel Membership**.

- Monthly: Cancellation takes effect at end of billing period.
- Annual: No prorated refund unless cancelled within 7 days of renewal.
