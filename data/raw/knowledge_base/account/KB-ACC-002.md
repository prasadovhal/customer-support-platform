# Creating and Verifying Your Account

## Creating a New Account

### Standard Account Registration

1. Visit acmestore.com and click **Sign Up**.
2. Enter your email address, first name, last name, and create a password.
3. Accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
4. Click **Create Account**.

A verification email is sent to your registered email address immediately.

### Password Requirements

Passwords must be:

- At least 10 characters
- Include at least one uppercase letter
- Include at least one number
- Include at least one special character (!@#$%^&*)
- Not reuse any of your last 5 passwords

### Account Types

| Account Type | How to Register |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Standard | Self-service at acmestore.com |
| Premium | Automatic upgrade (spending threshold) or manual request |
| Business | Contact sales or select "Business" during registration |
| Enterprise | Contact enterprise sales team |

## Email Verification

After registration, a verification email is sent with a link valid for 24 hours.

- Click the verification link to activate your account.
- Without verification, you can browse but cannot place orders.

If you did not receive the verification email:

1. Check spam/junk folder.
2. Go to **Account** > **Resend Verification Email**.
3. If still not received, contact support.

## Phone Number Verification

For additional security, you may optionally verify your phone number:

1. Go to **Account** > **Settings** > **Phone**.
2. Enter your phone number and click **Verify**.
3. Enter the 6-digit SMS code sent to your phone.

Phone verification enables SMS-based two-factor authentication.

## Account Limits Until Verified

Unverified accounts have restricted features:

- Cannot place orders above $50.
- Cannot use BNPL options.
- Cannot access Business pricing.
