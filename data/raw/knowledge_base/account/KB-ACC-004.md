# Privacy Settings and Data Management

## Overview

Acme Store is committed to protecting your personal data and providing you control over how your information is used. This article explains your privacy settings and data rights.

## Accessing Privacy Settings

Go to **Account** > **Privacy Settings** to manage:

- Email marketing preferences
- Personalized product recommendations
- Order data usage for analytics
- Third-party data sharing
- Cookie preferences

## Data We Collect

| Data Category | What We Collect | Why |
|--------------|-----------------|-----|
| Account data | Name, email, phone | Account management |
| Order data | Items, prices, addresses | Order fulfillment |
| Behavior data | Browse history, clicks | Personalization |
| Device data | IP address, browser type | Security, analytics |
| Payment data | Tokenized card info | Payment processing |

## Your Data Rights

Under applicable privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA), you have the right to:

- **Access**: Request a copy of all data we hold about you.
- **Rectification**: Correct inaccurate personal data.
- **Erasure**: Request deletion of your account and data.
- **Portability**: Receive your data in a machine-readable format.
- **Opt-out**: Opt out of marketing and data analytics.

## Submitting a Data Request

Submit requests at acmestore.com/privacy or email privacy@acmestore.com with:

- Full name
- Registered email
- Type of request
- Identity verification document (for deletion/access requests)

Response time: up to 30 days (per legal requirement).

## Data Deletion

Account deletion (see KB-ACC-001) triggers:

- Personal data deletion within 30 days.
- Order data anonymization (retained for 7 years per tax law).
- Removal from all marketing lists within 48 hours.

## Cookie Management

On your first visit, you are asked to consent to cookies. You can update preferences at any time via the **Cookie Settings** link in the website footer.

| Cookie Type | Purpose | Can Opt Out? |
|-------------|---------|--------------|
| Essential | Login, cart, security | No |
| Analytics | Traffic analysis | Yes |
| Marketing | Ad targeting | Yes |
| Preferences | Site settings | Yes |
