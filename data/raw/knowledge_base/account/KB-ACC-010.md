# Account Deletion and Data Removal

## Overview

You may request deletion of your Acme Store account at any time. This article explains the process, what data is removed, and what data is retained.

## Before You Delete Your Account

Consider:

- **Order history**: All visible order history will be removed from your account.
- **Store credit and gift card balance**: Any unused balance is forfeited unless transferred first.
- **AcmeCare plans**: Extended warranty coverage may be voided (contact support to transfer).
- **Active orders**: All pending orders must be received or resolved before deletion.
- **Subscriptions**: Cancel any active subscriptions before deleting.

## How to Delete Your Account

1. Resolve any open orders, returns, or refunds.
2. Use any remaining store credit balance.
3. Go to **Account** > **Settings** > **Delete Account**.
4. Confirm your password.
5. Complete 2FA verification (if enabled).
6. Read and accept the deletion terms.
7. Click **Permanently Delete Account**.

Alternatively, email privacy@acmestore.com with subject "Account Deletion Request" and your registered email.

## What Is Deleted

The following personal data is deleted within 30 days of your deletion request:

- Name, email, phone number
- Delivery addresses
- Payment method tokens
- Browse history and wishlists
- Support chat and ticket history
- Personalization preferences

## What Is Retained (Anonymized)

Data required for legal and financial compliance is retained in anonymized form:

| Data Type | Retention Period | Format |
|-----------|-----------------|--------|
| Order records | 7 years | Anonymized |
| Payment records | 7 years | Anonymized |
| Fraud/security records | 5 years | Anonymized |
| Return records | 3 years | Anonymized |

Anonymized data cannot be linked back to you and is used only for aggregate reporting.

## Account Reactivation

Account deletions are permanent and cannot be undone. If you wish to return to Acme Store, you must create a new account. Guest orders placed with the same email address cannot be linked to a new account.
