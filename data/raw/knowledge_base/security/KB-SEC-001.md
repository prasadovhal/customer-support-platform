# Account Security Best Practices

## Overview

Protecting your Acme Store account protects your personal information, payment data, and order history. This article outlines the most effective practices for keeping your account secure.

## Strong Password Hygiene

1. Use a **unique password** for Acme Store — never reuse passwords across sites.
2. Create a password of at least 12 characters mixing letters, numbers, and symbols.
3. Use a **password manager** (e.g., 1Password, Bitwarden) to generate and store complex passwords.
4. Never share your password with anyone — including people claiming to be Acme Store support.

Acme Store will **never** ask for your password in an email, chat, or phone call.

## Enable Two-Factor Authentication

2FA is the single most effective way to protect your account from unauthorized access. Even if your password is stolen, 2FA prevents login without the second factor.

Enable 2FA at: **Account** > **Security** > **Two-Factor Authentication**.

See KB-ACC-003 for setup instructions.

## Monitor Your Account Activity

Regularly review:

- **Order history**: Check for orders you did not place.
- **Login activity**: Go to **Account** > **Security** > **Recent Login Activity**.
- **Saved payment methods**: Verify no unauthorized payment methods have been added.

If you see suspicious activity, change your password immediately and contact support.

## Recognize Secure vs. Unsecure Connections

- Always access Acme Store via **https://acmestore.com** (note the HTTPS and padlock in the browser).
- Avoid accessing your account on public Wi-Fi without a VPN.
- Log out of your account when using shared or public computers.

## Device Security

- Keep your device's operating system up to date.
- Use device-level authentication (PIN, Face ID, fingerprint) to prevent unauthorized access.
- Install security software on Windows computers.

## If You Suspect a Compromise

1. Change your password immediately.
2. Review and revoke any suspicious login sessions: **Account** > **Security** > **Active Sessions**.
3. Contact Acme Store security: security@acmestore.com.
4. Contact your bank if payment data may be involved.
