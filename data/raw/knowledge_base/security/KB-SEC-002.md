# Recognizing Phishing and Scams

## Overview

Scammers frequently impersonate Acme Store to steal account credentials, payment information, and personal data. This article helps you identify and avoid these attacks.

## What Acme Store Will NEVER Do

Acme Store will **never**:

- Ask for your password via email, chat, or phone.
- Ask for your full credit card number (only the last 4 digits in customer service contexts).
- Request remote access to your computer.
- Offer unexpected prizes or gift cards requiring personal information.
- Send unsolicited requests to verify your account by clicking a suspicious link.

## Common Phishing Tactics

### Fake Order Confirmation Emails

Scammers send fake order confirmation emails with inflated prices, hoping you'll click a "Cancel Order" link that leads to a credential-stealing page.

**How to spot it**:
- The email sender is not @acmestore.com.
- The order number is not in your account history.
- The "Cancel" link goes to a non-Acme domain.

**What to do**: Go directly to acmestore.com (type it in the browser) and check your order history.

### Fake Refund or Prize Emails

"You're owed a refund of $XXX — click here to claim" — these lead to phishing sites.

**Acme Store refunds are credited automatically** — we never require you to "claim" them.

### Fake Customer Support Phone Calls

Scammers call pretending to be Acme Store support, claiming your account has been compromised, and asking you to verify your credentials or allow remote access.

**What to do**: Hang up and call the official Acme Store support number: 1-800-ACME-123.

### SMS Phishing (Smishing)

Fake delivery or order alerts via SMS with links to fraudulent sites.

**What to do**: Do not click links in unexpected SMS messages. Check your order status directly in your account or the app.

## How to Verify Acme Store Communications

Legitimate Acme Store emails:
- Always come from @acmestore.com (no variations like @acmestoree.com or @acme-store.net).
- Include your name (not just "Dear Customer").
- Include your partial order number or last 4 digits of payment method for purchase-related emails.

## Reporting Phishing

If you receive a suspicious message claiming to be from Acme Store:

- Forward the email to phishing@acmestore.com.
- Report the phone number or SMS to your carrier.
- File a report at ftc.gov/reportfraud.
