# Data Privacy and Customer Rights

## Overview

Acme Store is committed to transparent data practices. This article outlines how customer data is handled, stored, and protected.

## Data We Collect

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|----------|---------|---------|
| Identity | Name, email, phone | Account and order management |
| Financial | Tokenized card data | Payment processing |
| Order | Items, shipping addresses | Fulfillment and customer service |
| Behavioral | Browse history, search terms | Personalization and analytics |
| Technical | IP address, device type, browser | Security and service improvement |

Acme Store does **not** sell personal data to third parties for advertising purposes.

## How Data Is Protected

- **Encryption**: All customer data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).
- **Access controls**: Only authorized employees with a business need can access customer data.
- **Audit logging**: All internal access to customer data is logged and reviewed.
- **Data minimization**: We collect only what is needed for the stated purpose.

## Your Rights Under Privacy Laws

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

| Right | Description | How to Exercise |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| Access | See all data we hold | Submit a Data Access Request |
| Correction | Fix inaccurate data | Update in Account Settings or contact support |
| Deletion | Delete your account and data | Account > Delete Account (see KB-ACC-010) |
| Portability | Receive data in machine-readable format | Submit a Data Export Request |
| Restriction | Limit how data is processed | Contact privacy@acmestore.com |
| Objection | Object to certain processing | Privacy Settings or contact privacy team |

## Submitting a Privacy Request

Email: privacy@acmestore.com

Include:
- Full name and registered email
- Type of request
- Proof of identity (government-issued ID or recent order number)

Response time: Within 30 days (legal requirement under GDPR/CCPA).

## Third-Party Data Sharing

Acme Store shares data with:

- **Payment processors**: For transaction processing (data is tokenized).
- **Shipping carriers**: Name and delivery address only.
- **Analytics providers**: Anonymized/aggregated behavioral data.
- **Law enforcement**: Only when legally required by court order.

## Cookie Policy

See KB-ACC-004 for detailed cookie management. You can withdraw cookie consent at any time via the Cookie Settings link in the footer.
