# Unauthorized Account Access — Response Guide

## Overview

If you believe someone else has accessed your Acme Store account without your permission, take immediate action to secure your account and limit damage.

## Step 1: Assess the Situation

Signs of unauthorized access:

- Orders you did not place.
- Delivery addresses changed to unknown locations.
- Payment methods added or removed.
- Password or email changed without your action.
- Login alerts from unknown locations.

## Step 2: Secure Your Account Immediately

### If You Can Still Log In

1. Change your password immediately: **Account** > **Security** > **Change Password**.
2. Enable 2FA if not already enabled (KB-ACC-003).
3. Go to **Account** > **Security** > **Active Sessions** and revoke all sessions except your current one.
4. Review and remove any unauthorized saved addresses or payment methods.

### If You Cannot Log In (Password Changed)

1. Use "Forgot Password" to reset via your registered email.
2. If your email was also changed, contact support immediately at security@acmestore.com.
3. Provide identity verification documents to prove account ownership.

## Step 3: Review and Report Damage

1. Check order history for unauthorized orders (contact support to cancel/halt if still processing).
2. Verify no payment methods were used for unauthorized purchases.
3. Check if personal information (name, email, address) was changed.

## Step 4: Contact Acme Store Security

Email: security@acmestore.com
Phone: 1-800-ACME-123 > Press 3 for Security

Provide:
- Your account email
- Approximate time you noticed the breach
- List of unauthorized actions observed

Response time: Within 4 business hours for security incidents.

## Step 5: Contact Your Bank

If unauthorized purchases were made:

- Contact your bank to dispute charges.
- Request a new card number if it was stored in the account.

## Step 6: Check Other Accounts

If you use the same password elsewhere, change those passwords immediately. Use a password manager to generate and store unique passwords.

## What Acme Store Does in Response

- Temporarily locks the account to prevent further unauthorized activity.
- Investigates the access pattern.
- Reverses unauthorized orders where possible.
- Provides a security incident report upon request.
