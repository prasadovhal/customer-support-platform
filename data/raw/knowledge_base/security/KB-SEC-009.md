# Children's Privacy and Parental Controls

## Overview

Acme Store complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and similar international regulations. This article explains how we handle minors' data and how parents can control their children's access.

## Minimum Age Requirement

Acme Store accounts require users to be **18 years or older** (or the age of majority in their jurisdiction). Minors may use Acme Store products purchased by their parents or guardians, but may not create accounts independently.

If we discover an account was created by someone under 13, the account is immediately suspended and all data is deleted.

## Parental/Guardian Account Management

Parents may create and manage accounts for family use:

1. Create your own account (you must be 18+).
2. Place orders on behalf of family members.
3. Use purchase PIN (see below) to prevent unauthorized purchases.

## Purchase PIN

To prevent children from making unauthorized purchases using your account:

1. Go to **Account** > **Security** > **Purchase PIN**.
2. Set a 6-digit PIN.
3. When enabled, all purchases require the PIN.

This is especially useful if children have access to family devices.

## Family Devices and Account Sharing

If a child uses a family device:

- Do not save your Acme Store password in shared browsers.
- Use a separate browser profile for children's browsing.
- Use parental control software to restrict access to e-commerce sites if needed.

## Reporting Unauthorized Child Accounts

If you believe a child has created an account:

- Email privacy@acmestore.com with subject "Child Account Report".
- Provide: the email address or username used.
- We will verify and promptly delete the account.

## Kids Product Category

Acme Store features a Kids section (age-appropriate devices, educational tablets, toys). Products in this category do not collect additional data beyond standard order information.
