# Compliance and Regulatory Information

## Overview

This article provides information about Acme Store's compliance with relevant security and privacy regulations. This is primarily relevant to Business and Enterprise customers who require compliance documentation.

## Certifications and Standards

| Certification | Scope | Renewal |
|--------------|-------|---------|
| PCI DSS Level 1 | Payment card processing | Annual audit |
| SOC 2 Type II | Security, availability, confidentiality | Annual audit |
| ISO 27001 | Information security management | 3-year certification |
| GDPR compliance | EU customer data protection | Ongoing |
| CCPA compliance | California consumer data rights | Ongoing |

## Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Enterprise customers may request a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) for GDPR compliance:

1. Email: compliance@acmestore.com with subject "DPA Request".
2. Provide company legal name and jurisdiction.
3. DPA is provided within 5 business days.

The DPA outlines:
- Data processing scope and purposes.
- Subprocessors used by Acme Store.
- Data subject rights and how Acme Store facilitates them.
- Security measures in place.
- Data breach notification procedures (72-hour notification per GDPR Article 33).

## Data Breach Notification Policy

In the event of a data breach that affects customer personal data:

- **Enterprise customers**: Notified within 24 hours of breach confirmation.
- **All affected customers**: Notified within 72 hours (GDPR requirement) or as required by applicable state laws.
- **Public disclosure**: After law enforcement clearance and customer notification.

Notifications include: nature of the breach, data categories affected, steps taken, and recommended customer actions.

## Subprocessors

Acme Store uses the following categories of subprocessors:

- Payment processors (PCI DSS compliant).
- Cloud infrastructure providers (SOC 2 certified).
- Analytics platforms (data anonymization enforced).
- Email service providers (EU-US Data Privacy Framework compliant).

A full subprocessor list is available to Enterprise customers upon request.

## Law Enforcement Requests

Acme Store responds to valid legal processes (court orders, subpoenas, search warrants). We:

- Verify the legal validity of all requests.
- Notify affected customers where legally permitted.
- Publish a transparency report annually summarizing request volumes.
