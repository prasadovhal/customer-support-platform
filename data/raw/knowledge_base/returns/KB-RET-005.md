# Standard Return Policy — Version 1.0 (Historical)

> **NOTICE: This policy has expired.**
> This document represents the return policy in effect from **January 1, 2025** through **December 31, 2025**.
> For the current policy effective January 1, 2026, see KB-RET-001.

**Version 1.0 — Effective January 1, 2025 — Expired December 31, 2025**

## Overview

This is the historical version of the Acme Store Standard Return Policy, preserved for reference for orders placed during the 2025 calendar year.

## Return Window (2025 Policy)

Under the 2025 policy:

- **Standard products:** 30-day return window from confirmed delivery date
- **Electronics (laptops, smartphones, tablets):** 15-day return window
- **Damaged or defective items:** 45-day window (note: this was extended to 60 days in the 2026 policy)

## Key Differences from Current (2026) Policy

| Aspect | 2025 Policy (v1.0) | 2026 Policy (v2.0) |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Standard return window | 30 days | 30 days (unchanged) |
| Electronics return window | 15 days | 15 days (unchanged) |
| Damaged/defective window | 45 days | 60 days (extended) |
| Enterprise default window | 45 days | 45 days (unchanged) |
| Restocking fee (electronics) | Up to 20% | Up to 15% (reduced) |

## Applicability

This historical policy applies to:

- Orders placed between January 1, 2025 and December 31, 2025 (inclusive)
- Return requests initiated within the return window for those orders

Orders placed on or after January 1, 2026 are governed by the current policy (KB-RET-001 and KB-RET-002).

## Transition Cases

For orders placed in December 2025 where the return window extends into January 2026, the **2025 policy terms apply** since the order was placed under those terms.

If the 2026 policy is more favorable (e.g., longer damaged goods window), customers may request a goodwill exception by contacting support. Such exceptions are at Acme Store's discretion.

## Archive Note

This document is maintained as a historical record to support:

- Customer inquiries about policies applicable to 2025 orders
- Agent training and policy comparison
- Evaluation dataset accuracy (temporal policy questions)
- Compliance and audit requirements

Do not apply these terms to orders placed in 2026 or later.
