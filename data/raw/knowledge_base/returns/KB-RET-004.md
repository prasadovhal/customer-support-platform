# Enterprise Customer Return Terms

**Version 2.0 — Effective January 1, 2026**

## Overview

Enterprise customers (account segment: enterprise) operate under customized return terms negotiated at the time of account setup. This document describes the default enterprise return framework. Individual enterprise accounts may have additional or modified terms specified in their Master Service Agreement (MSA).

> **Check your MSA first.** If your enterprise account has a signed agreement, those terms take precedence over this default framework.

## Default Enterprise Return Window

In the absence of a specific MSA provision, enterprise customers are entitled to:

- **45 days** return window for all product categories (including electronics)
- **90 days** for bulk orders (10+ units of the same SKU)
- **90 days** for items confirmed as damaged or defective

This is an extended window compared to standard (30 days) and electronics (15 days) policies.

## Bulk Return Process

Enterprise accounts returning 5 or more items must use the Enterprise Returns Portal or contact their dedicated account manager directly.

Bulk returns are not processed through the standard returns interface in the customer account.

**Required documentation for bulk returns:**
- Purchase order (PO) number
- List of item serial numbers (for serialized products)
- Reason for return per unit or per SKU
- Contact person at the enterprise account

## Dedicated Support

Enterprise customers have access to:

- Dedicated account manager (check your welcome email)
- Priority return processing queue (target: 2 business day resolution vs. standard 5 days)
- On-site pickup for returns exceeding 20 units (arrange with account manager)
- Replacement units shipped before receipt of returned items (pre-authorization required)

## Restocking Fees

Standard restocking fees are waived for enterprise accounts unless explicitly stated in the MSA.

## Custom SLA

Enterprise accounts can negotiate custom return SLA terms including:

- Extended return windows beyond 90 days
- Automatic replacement without requiring return (for high-criticality operational equipment)
- Priority defect investigation with engineering team involvement

Contact your account manager or enterprise@acmestore.example to discuss custom terms.

## Dispute Resolution

Enterprise account disputes regarding returns not resolved through standard channels are escalated to the Enterprise Support Director within 48 hours.

## Important Notes

- Enterprise segment eligibility is managed by the Acme Store enterprise sales team.
- Self-service standard customers cannot access enterprise return terms by claiming enterprise status — verification is required.
- Attempting to misrepresent account segment to obtain extended return terms may result in account suspension.
