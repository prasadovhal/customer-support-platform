# Standard Return Policy

**Version 2.0 — Effective January 1, 2026**

## Overview

Acme Store offers a straightforward return policy designed to give customers confidence in their purchases. This document describes the standard return policy applicable to most product categories sold on the Acme Store platform.

## Return Window

Standard products may be returned within **30 days** of the confirmed delivery date.

> **Important:** Certain product categories have different return windows. See the Electronics Return Policy (KB-RET-002) for electronics-specific terms.

## Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for a standard return, the following conditions must be met:

- The return request must be initiated within 30 days of the confirmed delivery date.
- The item must be in its original condition — unused, unmodified, and with original packaging where possible.
- The original proof of purchase (order number) must be provided.
- The item must not be on the non-returnable list (see below).

## Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned under the standard policy:

- Digital downloads and software licenses once activated
- Perishable goods
- Custom-configured or personalized products
- Items marked "Final Sale" at the time of purchase
- Hazardous materials

## Return Process

1. Log in to your Acme Store account and navigate to **Order History**.
2. Select the order containing the item you wish to return.
3. Click **Initiate Return** and select the reason for the return.
4. A prepaid return shipping label will be emailed within 24 hours.
5. Pack the item securely and affix the return label.
6. Drop off at any authorized carrier location within 7 days of receiving the label.

## Refund Timeline

Once the returned item is received and inspected:

- Refunds are processed within **3–5 business days**.
- Credit card refunds appear within **5–10 business days** depending on the issuing bank.
- PayPal refunds are typically processed within **3–5 business days**.

## Customer Segments

Standard and premium customers are subject to the standard 30-day return window described in this document.

Business and enterprise customers may have different return terms negotiated under their account agreement. See KB-RET-004 for enterprise return terms.
