# Damaged and Defective Product Returns

**Version 2.0 — Effective January 1, 2026**

## Overview

Acme Store takes product quality seriously. If you receive a damaged or defective item, we provide an extended return window and prioritized resolution compared to our standard return policy.

> **Key distinction:** This policy applies specifically to items that are damaged in transit or defective upon arrival (DOA). It does not apply to items damaged through customer use.

## Extended Return Window

Damaged or defective items may be returned within **60 days** of the confirmed delivery date.

This applies regardless of the product category — including electronics, which normally have a 15-day standard return window.

## What Qualifies as Damaged or Defective?

### Damaged in Transit

- Visible physical damage to the product (dents, cracks, shattered screens)
- Damaged packaging that has compromised the product
- Missing components that should have been included
- Water or liquid damage from transit

### Defective (Dead on Arrival — DOA)

- Device does not power on
- Critical functions are non-operational from first use
- Manufacturing defects (assembly errors, component failures)
- Software/firmware preventing basic operation not resolvable by standard troubleshooting

### Does NOT Qualify

- Damage caused by customer misuse, drops, or accidents
- Normal wear and tear
- Issues arising after extended use (these may fall under warranty — see KB-WAR-001)
- Software issues not related to manufacturing defects

## Evidence Requirements

For damaged or defective returns, you will be asked to provide:

1. **Photos or video** of the damage or defect (required for claims over $100)
2. **Order number**
3. **Brief description** of the defect or damage
4. **Confirmation** that the item has not been intentionally modified

## Resolution Options

For damaged or defective items within the 60-day window, customers may choose:

| Option | Timeline |
|--------|----------|
| Full refund to original payment method | 3–5 business days processing |
| Replacement product (same model) | 2–5 business days shipping |
| Store credit (105% of item value) | Immediate upon approval |

No restocking fee applies to damaged or defective returns.

Acme Store covers return shipping costs for all damaged and defective returns.

## Priority Handling

Damaged and defective return cases are handled with priority. Initial response target: **4 business hours**.

For high-value items (over $500), cases are reviewed by a senior agent.

## Enterprise Customers

Enterprise customers with damaged or defective goods should contact their dedicated account manager or enterprise support line. Enterprise-tier SLAs may provide faster resolution.

## Contact

Report damaged or defective items via:

- Help Center: Account > Orders > Report Issue
- Email: returns@acmestore.example (include order number and photos)
- Chat: Available 24/7
