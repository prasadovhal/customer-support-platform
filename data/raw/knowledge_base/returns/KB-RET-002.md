# Electronics Return Policy

**Version 2.0 — Effective January 1, 2026**

## Overview

Due to the nature of electronic products — including data storage, software activation, and resale value — Acme Store applies a shorter return window to electronics compared to the standard return policy.

## Affected Product Categories

This policy applies to:

- Laptops
- Smartphones
- Tablets
- Smart displays
- E-readers

> **Note:** Accessories (cases, cables, chargers) and audio products follow the standard 30-day return window unless otherwise noted.

## Return Window

Electronics products must be returned within **15 days** of the confirmed delivery date.

This is a strict cutoff. Returns initiated after the 15-day window will not be accepted under this policy unless the item is damaged or defective (see KB-RET-003 for damaged/defective returns).

## Eligibility Requirements

- Return initiated within 15 calendar days of delivery confirmation.
- Device must not have been activated with a carrier (for smartphones).
- Device must not have been registered under a third-party platform (e.g., device management software).
- Factory reset must be completed before return — Acme Store is not responsible for personal data left on returned devices.
- Original packaging is strongly recommended but not strictly required if the item is in resalable condition.

## Opened vs Unopened

- **Unopened electronics:** Eligible for full refund within 15 days.
- **Opened electronics:** Eligible for return within 15 days if item is in functional, undamaged condition. A restocking fee of up to 15% may apply at Acme Store's discretion for opened electronics returned for non-defect reasons.

## Data Privacy Reminder

Customers are solely responsible for removing personal data from electronic devices before returning them. Acme Store will perform a factory reset on all returned electronics as part of the processing procedure, but cannot guarantee recovery of data left on returned devices.

## Exceptions

- **Defective items:** If an electronics item is defective upon arrival or becomes defective within the return window, no restocking fee applies. See KB-RET-003.
- **Enterprise customers:** May be subject to different terms under their account agreement. See KB-RET-004.
- **Damaged in transit:** If the item arrived physically damaged, the 60-day damaged goods policy applies. See KB-RET-003.

## Comparison with Standard Policy

| Aspect | Standard Products | Electronics |
|--------|------------------|-------------|
| Return window | 30 days | 15 days |
| Restocking fee | None | Up to 15% (opened) |
| Data responsibility | N/A | Customer |
| Defective exception | Yes | Yes |
