# Gift Returns Policy

## Overview

Acme Store allows recipients of gifts purchased through the platform to return or exchange items, even when the original purchaser's information is not shared.

## Eligibility

- The gift must have been purchased on Acmestore.com.
- The return must be initiated within the standard return window (30 days for most products, 15 days for electronics), measured from the original order delivery date.
- The item must be in original, unused condition with all accessories and packaging.

## How Gift Returns Work

### Option 1: Gift Receipt Return

If the gift came with a gift receipt, the recipient can:

1. Visit the Returns portal at acmestore.com/returns.
2. Enter the gift receipt code (printed on the slip).
3. Select the item to return.
4. Receive a **store credit** equal to the purchase price.

**Note:** The original purchaser is **not notified** of the return.

### Option 2: No Gift Receipt

If no gift receipt is available, the recipient must:

1. Contact customer support.
2. Provide the order number (if known) or approximate purchase date.
3. Verify the delivery address matches their address.

Store credit will be issued upon return confirmation.

## Gift Returns and Refunds

Gift returns are refunded as **store credit only**, not to the original payment method. This protects the purchaser's payment privacy.

## Exchanges for Gifts

Gifts can be exchanged for:

- A different size or color of the same product (subject to availability).
- A different product of equal or lesser value.
- The difference is applied as store credit for lesser-value exchanges.

## Exceptions

- Non-returnable items (see KB-RET-006) are also non-returnable as gifts.
- Items must be complete with original accessories.
