# Return Policy for Refurbished Products

## Overview

Acme Store sells certified refurbished products at discounted prices. These products are tested, cleaned, and restored to working order. The return policy for refurbished products differs slightly from new items.

## Return Window

| Customer Segment | Return Window |
|-----------------|---------------|
| Standard | 15 days from delivery |
| Premium | 30 days from delivery |
| Business | 30 days from delivery |
| Enterprise | Per contract (typically 30-45 days) |

**Note:** All refurbished electronics (regardless of customer segment) have a 15-day return window for standard customers, matching the new electronics policy.

## Condition Requirements

Refurbished products must be returned in the same condition as received:

- No additional scratches, cracks, or physical damage beyond what was disclosed at time of sale.
- All included accessories (cables, adapters, documentation) must be returned.
- The product must be factory reset / data wiped before return.

## Refurbishment Grade Disclosures

Acme Store grades refurbished products as:

| Grade | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Grade A | Like new, minimal cosmetic wear |
| Grade B | Light cosmetic marks, fully functional |
| Grade C | Visible cosmetic defects, fully functional |

If a product's condition is significantly worse than the disclosed grade, you may return it under the damaged item exception (see KB-RET-003).

## Warranty on Refurbished Products

All refurbished products sold by Acme Store include a **90-day warranty** covering manufacturing defects. This is shorter than the standard 1-year warranty for new products (see KB-WAR-001).

## How to Initiate a Refurbished Product Return

1. Log in to your account.
2. Go to **Orders** > Select the refurbished order.
3. Click **Return Item** and select the appropriate reason.
4. Follow the return shipping instructions (see KB-RET-008).
