# Digital Products and Software Returns

## Overview

Digital products, software licenses, and downloadable content are subject to a different return policy than physical goods due to the nature of digital delivery.

## General Policy

**Digital products are non-returnable once delivered or activated.**

This applies to:

- Software license keys
- Downloadable games and applications
- Digital books and media
- Online course access codes
- Cloud storage subscriptions (once activated)
- Streaming service vouchers

## Exceptions Where Returns Are Accepted

Despite the general no-return rule, Acme Store will issue a refund for digital products in the following cases:

### 1. Non-Delivery

If the license key or download link was not delivered within 24 hours of purchase, contact support. We will re-deliver or issue a full refund.

### 2. Invalid or Already-Used License Keys

If the license key provided is invalid or has already been activated by another user, we will replace it or issue a full refund.

### 3. Product Mismatch

If the digital product delivered does not match the description (e.g., wrong edition, wrong language), you are eligible for a replacement or refund.

### 4. Technical Defects

If the software has a critical technical defect that prevents basic usage and the developer has not issued a patch within 30 days, a refund may be processed.

## Subscription Cancellations

For recurring subscriptions purchased through Acme Store:

- Cancel any time from your account under **Subscriptions**.
- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
- No prorated refunds are issued for mid-period cancellations.
- Annual subscriptions cancelled within 7 days of renewal receive a full refund.

## Requesting a Digital Product Refund

Email digitalsupport@acmestore.com with:

- Order number
- License key (if applicable)
- Description of the issue

Response time: 1-2 business days.
